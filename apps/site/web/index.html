Why it's different

Built for the way a real shop actually trades.

Most retail software assumes a perfect connection and a head-office mindset. This one assumes a busy floor, a patchy line, and a Saturday before Diwali.

Sells with no internet

Every lane bills to its own disk first and reconciles later. A dead router or a cloud outage never closes a till.

One truth, every channel

Product, price, stock, customer and loyalty are the same at the counter, in the app and on a delivery — never three different answers.

GST, done right

Tax invoices with CGST, SGST and IGST, e-invoice (IRN), e-way bills and GSTR-1 — built in, not bolted on.

Risks, not noise

Approvals, variances and sync lag surface as clear exceptions to act on. Everything that's fine just runs, quietly.

Billing

Automatic monthly billing, the RBI-approved way.

You set it up once. After that, the monthly fee is collected on its own — with a reminder before every debit, exactly as India's e-mandate rules require.

Approve once

Set up UPI Autopay (or a bank e-mandate) with a single one-time approval on your phone.

We give notice

Before each month's debit, you get a pre-debit notification — no surprises on the statement.

It debits itself

The fee is collected automatically. Under ₹15,000 a month, there's no OTP to chase each time.

Cancel anytime

Give notice and service continues to the end of the paid month. Your data is never deleted.

Your card details are never stored. Billing runs on a secure mandate from your own bank or UPI app — we only ever hold a reference to it, never a card number.
