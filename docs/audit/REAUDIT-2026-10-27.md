## Owner decisions (answered 27 Oct 2026, in writing: D-1 A = OB-401, D-2 A = OB-402, D-3 B = OB-403)

**D-1: a wrong store-computer clock and new prices or offers (§31.1, extends OB-327)**
- **A (recommended).** At the 5-minute flag, keep current prices and offers and start no new price, offer or bill-year change until the clock is right. Alert head office and the owner. Selling never stops; a planned change may start late on that box.
- **B.** The same hold, but only at a larger gap the owner sets (for example 15 minutes). Fewer late starts, but a price can start up to that much early or late.
- **C.** Keep it as built and accept it in writing as an exception to §31.1. Nothing to build, but a wrong clock can start or end an offer at the wrong time.

**D-2: supplier rebate claims (D03-FR-03)**
- **A (recommended).** Build a claim document: finance approves it, it is sent to the supplier (printed or on the supplier portal) and matched to the payment. Unanswered claims show as overdue. Size S.
- **B.** Accept in writing that accrual plus payment matching is the claim, with claims made by phone or letter outside the system. Nothing to build, but there is no record of when a claim was made.
- **C.** Defer the claim document in writing to a named release (for example R6).

**D-3: the named privacy and security reviewer (OB-330, §8.2; blocks D14-FR-05 and R8-7)**
OB-330 chose "owner + named security reviewer" but nobody is named yet. Until someone is, the shelf-label, RFID, sensor and camera switches stay off, and nothing critical or high can be accepted as a risk.
- **A.** An outside security consultant: independent, costs a fee; cameras stay off until reviewed.
- **B.** The IT custodian Mr Sivakumar (already named for release signing, OB-342) as interim reviewer: free and quick, less independent; an outside review can still be added before any camera goes live.
- **C.** Name nobody yet: those switches stay off everywhere; the R8 innovation items cannot pass their release check.
