## Confirmed by hand (spot checks of the most serious findings)

- **M04-FR-01, misleading screen (P-08).** The Range tab's "take out of the range" (`apps/web-erp/web/merchandising.js` `drop-item`) calls `session.drop()`, which decides locally and saves nothing. The screen says "Taken out of the range".
- **M05-FR-03, the Offer tab's Start cannot work for real.** No screen defines an offer, and head office refuses an undefined offer (`services/pricing/src/promotions.ts`, `promotion_not_defined`). Its browser test passes against a stub.
- **M07-FR-04, freight on supplier bills.** `apps/web-erp/src/supplier-bills-session.ts` never sends `charges`, and the e-invoice reader ignores `OthChrg`. A bill with freight is refused as "does not add up".
- **AI-SCREEN.** The AI page (`apps/web-erp/src/ai-control.ts`, `web/ai.js`) can stop agents and record incidents, but it cannot switch an agent on or set budgets (`PUT /v1/ai/agents/enabled`, `PUT /v1/ai/budget`). In real use every agent stays off.
