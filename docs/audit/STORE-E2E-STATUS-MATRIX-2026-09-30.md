## 5. What is genuinely proven today, in one paragraph

A served till takes a sale offline, writes it to disk, the store box relays it to the cloud on real PostgreSQL, stock
falls, and a refund follows the same path; a manager closes the day through the box; the buyer raises a purchase order
and captures an invoice from a screen that reaches head office; goods are received on the cloud with quarantine rules
and reviewed on a screen; the handhelds receive, put away and pick against served data and queue durably on the device;
promotions, price changes, expiry/recall, write-offs and the cash-office sign-off each reach head office from their
screens; **since SP-1/SP-2a/SP-2b/SP-3a (30 Sep):** a rejected sync conflict is a visible dead-letter; a manager's approval
decision, delivery and blind count entered on the served screen are durable on the device, handed to the box, held on
its fsync'd log and recorded at head office with the decider / receiver / counter re-verified and the count's expected
figure, value and threshold computed there; and the WAREHOUSE handheld — enrolled once on the box's authenticated device
socket with a one-time head-office code — hands its put-aways, picks and receiving scans to the box and on to head
office, the worker re-verified, the cloud bin and the store's on-hand moving once — all surviving reload, restart, a cut
line and a lost reply; **and since SP-3b** the handheld counts a bin BLIND (head office compares it against ITS bin and
holds a material or unvalued variance) and raises an adjustment REQUEST that posts only when a supervisor who is not
the raiser approves it — once. **Not yet proven, and therefore the store-management workflow is NOT complete:** the
picker and driver handheld queues still reach nobody (SP-3c, behind the core-store slices); a held count variance or a
pending request is not yet approved-then-applied from the manager's own relayed decision (SP-4); approvers, stock and
values can still be typed on the direct routes; the shop-network leg to the handhelds has no TLS yet; transfers and
count corrections do not change ordinary stock; a receipt does not reduce the order; an invoice is not joined to its
order and receipt or posted as a liability; the floor indent chain does not exist; the till names a placeholder cashier
and day and cannot close its shift. No physical device, staff UAT or production verification has been performed.
