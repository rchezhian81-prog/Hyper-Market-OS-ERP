## 3. Owner decisions needed (each with options)

**D-1. Does an ordinary price change need a second person? (M05-FR-01; M05.md "change approved (maker-checker)")**
- A: Every price change needs a second person. Safest. Slower at busy times; the manager is asked many times a day.
- B: Only loss-making prices need a second person (as built), plus the fresh second-factor sign-in for bulk changes. Fast. One person can still move a profitable price without anyone checking.
- C: A second person when the change is above a set size (for example more than 10% or more than ₹50), or below the margin floor. A middle path; you set the number.

**D-2. When does an unanswered approval move up, and to whom? (M02-FR-03 "an aged pending approval escalates")**
- A: After 4 trading hours, to the store manager. After 8, to you.
- B: At the end of the trading day, straight to you.
- C: A different time for each kind (refund 1 hour, purchase order 24 hours, stock adjustment 48 hours), all to you, and each one changeable.

**D-3. The money limits for each role (M02-FR-02; OC-10/OC-11, AVR-08).** The software will hold them centrally either way. You give the numbers.
- A: Start at ₹0 for everyone but you, so every approval comes to you until you raise them.
- B: Start with a suggested ladder: cashier ₹0; store manager ₹5,000 refund / ₹25,000 purchase order / ₹2,000 adjustment; you unlimited. You change any figure.
- C: You fill in the table yourself before the build finishes.

**D-4. What "heat-map ready" means (M04-FR-04).**
- A: Data only: each shelf area gets floor coordinates, and an export of sales and margin per area (for a later picture). Small.
- B: A floor-plan heat-map screen in the web ERP, coloured by margin per square foot. Larger.
- C: A now, and B named for a later release in writing.

**D-5. How the till knows which bank a card belongs to for a bank offer (M05-FR-03).**
- A: Read it from the card machine's answer (card number range). Needs the payment provider (outside party); exact.
- B: The cashier picks the bank on screen and the approval number is kept. Works without the provider; open to mistakes or misuse.
- C: Bank offers stay off until a provider is connected, named in writing for a release.

**D-6. What "central" and "local" buying mean at SRE (M06-FR-04).**
- A: Central = head office buys for the warehouse, then transfers to the stores. Local = a store manager buys for their own store under a limit.
- B: Central = head office buys and has it delivered straight to each store. Local as in A.
- C: Both A and B, chosen on each order.

**D-7. What a "bundle" is (M03-FR-02, M05-FR-03).**
- A: Its own product with its own barcode, made of named items. Selling it takes stock from each item.
- B: A price offer only ("these 3 together for ₹199"). No new product; stock moves item by item.
- C: Both.

**D-8. When nutrition facts are required (M03-FR-03).**
- A: Required before any packaged food can be published.
- B: Recorded when known, never blocking.
- C: Required only for food the shop packs or labels itself. Bought-in packs print their own.
