## 3. Owner decisions needed (each with options; none of these is invented: the roadmap leaves the choice open)

- **D-1. D09-FR-06 "contribution stop rules".** The roadmap says "stop rule"; the built engine only flags. (A) Flag only, as built: the order is taken and the owner sees it lost money. (B) Below the owner's threshold, a new order for that slot or zone needs a manager's approval. (C) Below the threshold, refuse delivery for that slot or zone and offer pickup.
