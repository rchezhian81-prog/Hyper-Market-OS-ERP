## Open owner decision found

**D-§31.1: what a store computer does with a new price or promotion while its clock is out.** OB-327 set the warning (2 minutes) and the flagged bills (5 minutes). It did not say whether a new price or offer may start while the clock is wrong, and §31.1 says it "blocks time-sensitive local activation".
- **A (recommended):** at the 5-minute "flag bills" level, the box keeps the prices and offers already running. It does not start any new price, offer or bill-year change until the clock is back in step, and it raises an alert to head office and the owner. Selling never stops. Consequence: a planned price change may start a few minutes or hours late on that till until someone fixes the PC clock.
- **B:** the same hold, but only at a larger difference that the owner sets (for example 15 minutes). Warnings and flagged bills stay at 2 and 5 minutes. Consequence: fewer delayed price starts, but a price can start up to 15 minutes early or late.
- **C:** keep it as built (warn and flag bills only; new prices still start on the box's clock), accepted in writing as an exception to §31.1. Consequence: no build work, but a wrong PC clock can start or end an offer at the wrong time without anything stopping it.
