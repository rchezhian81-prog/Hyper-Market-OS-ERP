## Open owner decisions

**1. D03-FR-03: does "funding claims" need a claim raised on the supplier?** The roadmap says "contracts, rebates, schemes and funding claims". Today the shop accrues what it has earned and matches money when it arrives, but never sends the supplier a claim.
- (a) Build a rebate claim: approved by Finance, sent to the supplier (printed or on the supplier portal), then matched to the receipt. A second person approves. Size S. The supplier sees exactly what the shop claims, and an unanswered claim shows as overdue.
- (b) Accept, in writing, that the accrual plus the receipt reconciliation counts as the claim, and that staff claim by phone or letter outside the system. Nothing to build, but there is no record of when a claim was made or of a supplier ignoring it.
- (c) Defer the claim document in writing to a named release (for example R6, with the supplier portal). The row stays open until then.
