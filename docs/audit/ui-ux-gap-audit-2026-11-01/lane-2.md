## The five findings that matter most in this lane (most serious first)
1. Two stock-moving buttons cannot be read: "Save the indent on this device" (Floor indents) and "Record the run" / "Save the weights" (Production) are dark words on a dark green button — one missing colour line on each page fixes it.
2. A manager must type codes a programmer knows and a shop person does not — location ids, product ids, item codes, branch codes, staff ids, approval ids — on count plans, packaging, scrap, store events, warehouse, dispatch, loss prevention and held returns; none offers a list to pick from.
3. "Record a loss" shows two whole forms for the same loss at once (online and line-down) and three filled buttons, so the person must guess which to fill; the page also asks the manager to type what the loss is worth, which head office should value.
4. Empty pages read as good news when head office simply holds nothing: Facilities says "every scheduled check is up to date" with no schedule, Shelves says "everything on the plan has been counted" under a banner that says there is no plan, Expiry says "nothing is close to its date" with no batches, and "Products nobody can sell" still shows sample data on a real store computer.
5. The Warehouse screen scrolls sideways on a phone and uses the information-blue tone as its action colour instead of the one emerald primary, with two filled buttons at once and a typed unit cost on a transfer the Transfers page says is never priced here.

## Product-wide patterns seen (one line each)
- Pages built before W1 use their own `button.act` styles instead of `.sre-btn-primary`; the sweep finds zero `.primary` on 26 of 29 pages, and several pages fill two or three buttons in the accent at once (scrap, packaging, warehouse, write-off).
- Typed ids instead of pick lists is the norm on every setup-style form in this lane (store, place, product, item, branch, staff, approval, case).
- "Head office refused:" followed by a raw code (`unreadable_answer`, HTTP status) when no sentence comes back — count-plans, transfers, packaging, scrap, floor-heat-map, gates, dispatch, related-parties share the same helper pattern; and "cannot be reached — nothing was read or saved" never names the next safe action.
- The page heading in the top bar (`h1.who`) stays English after the Tamil toggle on indents, waste, facilities, production, write-off-capture, held-returns, stock-health (the body translates; the chrome's heading line does not).
- Labels and hints at 13px, notes at 12px, on pages that predate the type scale (indents, production, stock-health, write-off-capture, facilities, unsellable).
- State words without the one icon set (plain text or ⚠/✓ characters) on transfers, count-plans, held-returns, indents, demand-events.
- Summary tiles draw "0" for a list that was really read and is really empty (count-plans, transfers, food-safety) — honest, but indistinguishable at a glance from an unknown; the design's "Not known" is used nowhere in this lane (sweep `notKnown` = 0 on every page).
- No tap-count test exists for any office action in this lane (R1 unmeasured everywhere).
- Every page carries the chrome badge; "store copy … old" freshness strip appears only on expiry (the one pack-fed page here).
