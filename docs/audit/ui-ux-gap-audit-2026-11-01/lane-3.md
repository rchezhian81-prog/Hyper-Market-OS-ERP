# Lane 3 — Finance, Reports, Customers (office pages)  (UI/UX gap audit, 1 Nov 2026)
**Pages:** 30 (29 pages; "Reconciliation" is the Month-end page opened on its payments tab). **Verdicts:** CLEAR 5 · ROUGH 22 · HARD 3.
**How checked:** every page's `apps/web-erp/web/<page>.html` + `.js` and its `apps/web-erp/src/*-session.ts` dictionary read (headings, controls, write handlers, failure wording, en/ta keys); the sweep rows for 29 of the 30 (online-store had not been reached by the sweep when this was written — judged from code and marked "code only"; cash-office, day-reopen, itc-review and return-governance arrived last and audit clean at desk and phone); 17 screenshots looked at (desk EN, phone EN). No script run.

**Two facts that shape most verdicts.** (1) None of the 29 pages uses the shared office parts of 1 Nov (W1): no `sreOffice.table/cards/field/chip` in any page script except the W1 touches on Customers, Reports and Month-end, and no `.sre-btn*` class in any HTML — every page draws its own buttons, lists and forms, so "one visually dominant primary action" is not marked anywhere (the sweep's `primaries` count is 0 on all 29; what looks primary is each page's own green `button`), and several pages show two to six green buttons at once. (2) Every financial write runs on one click of its button with no consequence line and no confirm step (the pages say "No prompt/confirm/alert" on purpose, but nothing on-screen replaced the browser dialog): posting a day to the accounts, recording supplier money, sending a campaign, publishing the online store, refunding at the desk, closing the month (the "ask for a signature" second person is the authority part; the confirm part is still missing). Reasons and a second approver are asked where §28 requires it — that is the authority rule, not the confirm rule.

## The five findings that matter most in this lane (most serious first)
1. The Morning brief tells the owner "Nothing needs your attention today" and "the figures above are complete and correct" on a morning when sales, margin and cash are "not available" — it must say "not known" instead (P-08).
2. Money moves on one click with nothing on screen saying what will happen or asking to confirm: posting a day to the accounts, recording supplier money, publishing the online store, sending a campaign, signing off a drawer — the design rule for financial actions is consequence, authority and a confirm step.
3. The Bill findings page shows 48 identical cards in one endless column — a manager cannot see what kind or how many; it needs counts by kind and a register with paging.
4. Twenty-three of these pages draw their own buttons and lists instead of the shared office parts, so nothing is marked as the one primary action and two to six green buttons compete on Customers, Loyalty, Campaigns, Tally files, Promotion funding and Month-end; and the one W1 page, Customers, scrolls sideways on a phone.
5. After the Tamil toggle, page titles (Day book, GST reconciliation, Stored-value oversight), every report's name on Reports, the Finance page's state sentence and head office's own findings stay English — a Tamil reader gets half a screen.

## Product-wide patterns seen (one line each)
- No lane-3 page uses `.sre-btn*` or `sreOffice.field/chip`; buttons are page-own `button` elements, so "one primary action" is unmarked product-wide and the sweep's `primaries` metric cannot see the real primary.
- Financial writes run on a single click; "No prompt/confirm/alert" was taken as "no confirm step" — the design wants an on-screen consequence + confirm sheet, not a browser dialog.
- The header line `h1.who` is a hard-coded English text node on the older box-served pages (day-book.html:68, cash-office.html:61, day-reopen.html:59, stored-value.html:62, gst-reconciliation, return-governance); the page's own `#title` h2 repaints on the toggle, the header line never does.
- Words that come from head office or the pack (report names, brief sentences, GST findings, role names, state `why` texts) are English only; the dictionaries only cover the page's own chrome words.
- Native `<input type="date">` everywhere shows the browser's locale (mm/dd in the demo), and several pages use free-text dates with a placeholder instead.
- "All clear" sentences exist on seven pages; most are guarded by a successful read, the Morning brief and Customer cases ("Every resolved case has a root cause.") are not.
- Disabled buttons never say why (loyalty for a cashier, tally-files for a manager, customers).
- Hint and footnote text under 14px on company-report, stored-value and (Lane 1's) pricing.
