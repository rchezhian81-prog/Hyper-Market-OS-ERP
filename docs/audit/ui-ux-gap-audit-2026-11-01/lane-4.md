## The five findings that matter most in this lane (most serious first)
1. **Users & roles shows the owner nothing** — "has not been told about any accounts", no sign-in list, and no way to give or take back a role — so the one screen that controls who can do what in the shop cannot be used by the owner.
2. **Store setup is unusable on a phone and misleading on a desk** — 26 tiny switches a thumb cannot hit, the page scrolls sideways, 26 greyed "Save" buttons with no reason, every question in English on the Tamil screen, and "0 / 26 settings chosen" on a shop that is already trading.
3. **Tills & devices says "Every device is up to date" when no device exists** — a page that reads all-clear with nothing registered, and offers no way to register a till.
4. **"Audit log" is not an audit log** — the menu promises one, the page is retention rules, and the menu offers it to the accountant whom the store computer then refuses.
5. **The migration page runs on sample data for the real owner** — it says "this is not your shop" and nothing can be signed, decided or rolled back from the hosted copy.

## Product-wide patterns seen (one line each)
- **People and stores are typed as ids** on store-tasks, delegation, certificates, adoption, appointment-letters, help — no pick list anywhere in this lane; a manager must know `u-ravi` and `S1`.
- **The page heading stays English on the Tamil screen** on ≥ 9 pages (checklist, rostering, fleet, integration-health, ess, my-payslip, payroll, document-templates …): the chrome draws `h1#who` from the page's first heading and the pages translate a child span, not the h1.
- **Every button is the same accent colour** — none of the 31 page files uses `.sre-btn-primary`; pages style `button` as the primary, so a screen with three actions has three primaries (help, schedule, approval-rules, admin).
- **13px words a person must read** in 26 of 31 page stylesheets (`.meta`, `.facts`, `.why`, `.hint`, `.tag`, error lines on data-io, field messages on setup) — against §3.2 "nothing under 14px".
- **None of the 34 pages uses the shared office parts** (`sreOffice.table/state/cards/field`): 14 raw `<table>`s, own empty lines, own forms — W1's register, states and fields reached none of People/Admin/Systems.
- **Menu name ≠ page name** (Rota/Rostering, Tills & devices/Devices, Connections/Integration health, Audit log/Records kept, Workforce = helper findings) — a person searching the rail lands on a page called something else.
- **Refusals say what head office said but not what to do next** — the shared `outcomeWords` pattern is "Head office refused: <reason>"; only the unreachable case adds "nothing was changed"; no refusal names a next safe action (§1.5 third part).
- **Raw ids on screen**: `u-cash`/`u-owner` as "who", `u-box`, `Asia/Kolkata`, role names in English — the person's name is never shown.
- **On the phone the chrome takes the first screenful** (breadcrumb, Pin, Find, Screens, badge, toggle) and the page name is repeated three times before the first task (my-tasks screenshot).
