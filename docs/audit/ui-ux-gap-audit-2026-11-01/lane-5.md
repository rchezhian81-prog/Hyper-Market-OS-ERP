## The five findings that matter most in this lane (most serious first)
1. On the till, everything that is not scan, qty, void, hold or tender lives behind one "More" button that opens a flat list of up to 22 choices — a new cashier must read the whole list to find "Refund" or "Member"; it needs the same dozen-headings treatment the office menu got.
2. The till cannot find an item by name or by a keyed barcode, so a torn label means the sale stops or the item is left off — the roadmap's "product search" screen does not exist at the till.
3. Taking the float or moving cash to the safe sends the amount the moment OK is tapped, with no "this will be recorded as…" line and no confirm; and a price cut goes to the manager's PIN before the manager is shown the cut and the limit.
4. The owner's phone says "No alerts today" and "Nothing needs a decision" on an empty list even when the phone has never synced or is hours old — the one screen where "nothing" must never read as "all clear".
5. Self-checkout has no connection badge and no unsent count while it is running, and its "It has not answered" outcome does not say whether the basket counts as paid or what the colleague should do next.

## Product-wide patterns seen (one line each)
- Every shell's English and Tamil dictionaries have the same keys (script over 20 files), but four static pages (concession tag, customer sign-in, site home, subscribe) print both languages at once with no toggle and `lang="en"` fixed.
- The offline "sample stand-in" pattern survives on the supplier and B2B portals (announced, but still a stand-in for an outside party), after the office pages dropped theirs in OB-380.
- Tap budgets are measured only where a Tally test was written (till core, refund lookup, handhelds, owner, customer, manager approvals); the money and closing flows (float, split tender, close till, self-checkout, queue till) have none.
- The owner-app, self-checkout, kiosk, queue and portal tests prove taps or words, not the rendered WCAG audit; only the till, handhelds, customer app, sign-in and office pages run `auditPage`.
- The sweep's shell rows and screenshots did not arrive before this lane closed; nothing above rests on them.
