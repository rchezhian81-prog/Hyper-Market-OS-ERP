# As-is processes — how the store works today

The current, real way the store operates — captured from the owner and staff, one
area at a time, in plain language. This is a **template of the questions to answer**;
fill each section from real observation during Stage 1. Do not assume — where the
answer is "it depends", write down what it depends on.

> Prepared during Stage 0. Completed in Stage 1 by walking the store and asking.

For each area below, capture: **who** does it, **what steps** in order, **what
system/paper** is used, **where approval** happens, **where it goes wrong / is
slow**, and **how long** it takes.

## 1. Purchasing
- How is a purchase decided (reorder trigger, manual judgement, scheme-driven)?
- Who can raise a PO, who approves, and above what value?
- How are supplier schemes, rebates and rate changes handled today?

## 2. Receiving (goods inward)
- How is a delivery counted and checked against the PO and the invoice?
- Batch, expiry, MRP and damage — captured how, and where?
- What happens on a short/over/wrong delivery?

## 3. Pricing
- Who sets and changes prices, and how is MRP vs selling price handled?
- How are promotions and markdowns run today?
- How is a below-cost or margin problem noticed?

## 4. Billing (POS)
- Step-by-step from scan to receipt; tenders accepted (cash/card/UPI/credit).
- What happens when the system or internet is slow or down?
- Suspends, returns, no-receipt returns, supervisor overrides.

## 5. Returns & exchanges
- Policy with and without a receipt; who approves; how refunds are given.

## 6. Cash close
- Till float, pickups, safe drops; shift and day close; over/short handling.
- Who reconciles cash, card, UPI, and against what?

## 7. Stock counting
- Cycle vs full counts; how variance is investigated and adjusted; who approves.
- How expiry and near-expiry stock is found and acted on today.

> Each area completed here feeds the **to-be** design (`to-be-processes.md`) and
> confirms the **baseline** numbers (`baseline.md`).
