# AVR closure — Audit Validation Required items

The roadmap (§13) carries **twenty AVR items** — things the audit flags as needing
validation before they can be relied on. Stage 1 is meant to close them, and
Annexure G finding A-11 adds the control: **a stage may not enter GO while an AVR
item mapped to it remains open** (to be written into §22).

Each AVR needs: exactly what to find out, who to ask, the answer, and its status.

> **⛔ roadmap.** The twenty specific AVR items are defined in roadmap §13, which is
> not yet in the repository. The register structure is ready; populate the twenty
> rows verbatim from §13 when the roadmap is added. Every AVR must have a **named
> person who will answer it** (Stage 1 acceptance).
