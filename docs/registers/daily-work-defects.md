# Daily-work defects — every role's daily work, done in a real browser (15 November 2026)

**What was run.** Each role's daily tasks were done by clicking through the real screens in headless Chromium (Playwright),
the way a person would: open the page from the office menu, fill the boxes, press the button, then check that the result
reached head office's records (stock, orders, bills, prices, approvals). The setup:

- Head office: its real routes and rules (`apiHarness()`, in memory), loaded with the pilot demo data (`db/seed/pilot`:
  owner, store manager, cashier, accountant, warehouse staff, 2 suppliers, an open order, opening stock). Three more
  departments were added: Dairy (milk 500 ml), Fruit & Veg (loose tomato by kg, scale label), Personal care (shampoo).
  The price list was published to the till.
- The store computer: a real store box (`startEdge`) serving the till and the office pages, taking its store setup and
  till pack from head office, syncing sales back, with till PINs for each person. The office pages reached head office
  as the signed-in person (`EDGE_SCREEN_TRUST_FORWARDED_USER`, `/v1` carried by the browser), as the existing browser
  tests do. This works around D-001 so the rest of the product could be seen.
- Store PC clock in this test was UTC. The "time zone is UTC" warning this caused is NOT listed.

**How to reproduce.** Copy the kept harness `zz-walkthrough-server.e2e.ts` (scratch folder, see the hand-over note) into
`tests/e2e/`. Build `web-erp`, `pos`, `warehouse-app` and `owner-app` with `node scripts/build-app.mjs <app>`. Run it with
`WALK_DIR=<folder> vitest run tests/e2e/zz-walkthrough-server.e2e.ts --config vitest.e2e.config.ts`. It writes
`<folder>/info.json` (box port, PINs) and serves head office on port 4790. Then drive the pages with the kept scripts
(`lib.mjs` opens a page as a person). Delete the harness file afterwards.

Severity: **Blocks** = the task cannot be done · **Hurts** = it can be done, but wrongly, with a workaround, or with a
real risk of a mistake · **Annoys** = jargon, clutter, extra steps.

## What worked (seen, checked at head office)

Till: PIN sign-in, opening float with a confirm, barcode scans, a weighing-scale label for loose tomato and loose rice
(right kg and price), quantity change (3 replaced 1), void with a reason, cash / UPI / card bills on screen with GST split,
refund from a bill number (stock went back), blind close with a short reason. Sales synced to head office (stock fell).
Over/short sign-off by a second person. Raising an order (with pickers) and issuing it by a second person. Receiving
against the order (short of 4 shown on Goods receipt review). Typing a bill with GST and matching it (as owner). The
owner's phone Today page (taken, payments by tender, by hour). Most screens switch to Tamil correctly.

- The real store PC with office PIN sign-in on (D-001). Every result above went through the test way round D-001.

  Bills were checked on screen only.

  count were not driven.
- Expiry markdowns and near-expiry stock: the demo batches are not near their dates, and new receipts carry no expiry (D-029).
- The owner's phone app over the internet, the customer, picker and delivery apps: not daily tasks of these six roles here.
