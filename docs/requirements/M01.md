# M01 — Organization, branch and configuration

- **Priority:** P0 (architectural/security foundation) · **Release:** R1 · **Stage:** 7 (built on the Stage 5 foundation)
- **Roadmap source:** §5 M01; cross-cutting §10, §28, §29, §31, §34, §35
- **Purpose:** The backbone every other module hangs on — the legal entities, branches, stores, warehouses and departments; the business calendar, currencies and number series; and safe, versioned configuration. If this is wrong, everything built on top inherits the error.

## M01-FR-01 — Organizational hierarchy

- **Statement:** The system shall model companies, GST registrations, branches, stores, warehouses and departments as a governed hierarchy, so that every transaction, permission and report can be scoped to an exact place in the business.
- **Business reason:** Multi-branch control, correct GST attribution, and branch/department-scoped permissions (§28) and reporting all depend on one authoritative structure.
- **Actors & scope:** Platform Admin (create/edit), Owner (approve structural change), all roles (consume scope). Company/branch boundaries per §28.
- **Preconditions:** At least one company and one GST registration exist before any branch; the legal structure is confirmed `⟳ AVR-01`.
- **Main flow:** Admin creates company → adds GST registration(s) → adds branch/store → adds warehouse(s) → adds departments → submits for owner approval → activated.
- **Alternate / error flows:** Duplicate legal entity or GSTIN rejected; a branch cannot be activated without a parent company and a GST registration; incomplete hierarchy saved as draft.
- **Business rules:** A GSTIN is unique and validated for format; every store belongs to exactly one company and one GST registration; a department belongs to one store; deactivating a node with active stock or open transactions is blocked (see FR-04).
- **Permissions / approval:** Only Platform Admin edits structure; structural changes are owner-approved (maker-checker, §28). No shared logins (§9.1 SEC-03).
- **Offline / sync (§31):** Read-only from signed versioned local cache at the edge; structure is cloud-approved and published to stores; in-flight transactions keep the version they referenced (§31.1).
- **Audit / privacy / security:** Every create/edit/activate/deactivate recorded immutably with who/what/when/where/before/after (M34-FR-01); no personal data in this entity.
- **Integration / reporting:** Exposed via API-01 (Identity/Admin); every report and export can filter by company/branch/warehouse/department.
- **Acceptance criteria:** A branch cannot be created without a company and GSTIN; a report filtered to one department returns only that department's data; an attempt to create a duplicate GSTIN is rejected with a clear message.

## M01-FR-02 — Business calendar, currency, number series and document templates

- **Statement:** The system shall define, per company/branch, the business/trading day, time zone, currency and fixed decimal precision, document number series, and versioned document templates.
- **Business reason:** Day-close totals, GST periods and shift reports must agree; money must be exact; documents must be numbered without gaps or duplicates. (Closes audit finding A-13.)
- **Actors & scope:** Platform Admin/Finance configure; all transactional modules consume.
- **Preconditions:** Organizational hierarchy exists (FR-01); store trading hours confirmed `⟳ AVR-02, AVR-07`.
- **Main flow:** Configure time zone and **trading-day cut-off** → currency and precision → number series per document type → upload/version document templates (receipt, invoice, PO, GRN, statement) → approve → publish.
- **Alternate / error flows:** A number series nearing exhaustion raises an alert; a template change is versioned, never overwritten; ambiguous trading-day config is rejected.
- **Business rules:** **The trading day is an explicit rule** (e.g. business day runs to a defined cut-off after midnight) applied consistently to day close, reporting and tax periods (§29.1, A-13); money carries explicit currency and fixed precision (§29.1); timestamps stored UTC, presented in store time zone (§29.1); number series are gap-free and unique per document type.
- **Permissions / approval:** Finance/Admin with owner approval for currency/precision and trading-day changes; changes are effective-dated.
- **Offline / sync (§31):** Fully available offline from local cache; the edge uses the published trading-day and number-series rules; offline document numbers use a reserved range per lane to prevent collisions, reconciled on sync.
- **Audit / privacy / security:** All calendar/number/template changes audited immutably; template versions retained per retention policy.
- **Integration / reporting:** API-01/API-11; every dated report honours the trading-day rule; GST periods derive from it.
- **Acceptance criteria:** A sale committed at 00:30 falls in the correct trading day per the configured rule; two lanes offline for a day produce no duplicate document numbers; a template change produces a new version while old documents keep their original layout.

## M01-FR-03 — Feature flags and configuration versioning with rollback

- **Statement:** The system shall manage feature flags and all configuration as versioned records with preview, approval, activation, and one-step rollback to a prior version.
- **Business reason:** Configuration is changed far more often than code; unsafe or unversioned config change is a common cause of outages. Rollback protects trading.
- **Actors & scope:** Platform Admin (edit), Owner/authorized approver (approve high-impact flags), all modules (read).
- **Preconditions:** Configuration service available (Stage 5 foundation).
- **Main flow:** Edit config/flag → preview effect → submit → approve → activate with effective date → (if needed) roll back to a named prior version.
- **Alternate / error flows:** A rejected change never activates; a failed activation auto-holds at the last-known-good version; conflicting concurrent edits are surfaced as an exception, not silently merged (§31.1).
- **Business rules:** Every configuration change is a new immutable version with author, reason and effective date; rollback restores a prior version as a new version (append-only, never destructive — §29.1); high-impact flags (payments, pricing, offline limits) require owner approval.
- **Permissions / approval:** Maker-checker; separation of duties — the maker cannot approve their own high-impact change (§28).
- **Offline / sync (§31):** Config is published as a signed versioned pack to the edge; stores retain last-known-good; a store never activates an unpublished/expired config.
- **Audit / privacy / security:** Full immutable history of who changed what, when, from and to (M34-FR-01); signed config packs (§35 "signed updates").
- **Integration / reporting:** API-01/API-11; a configuration-history report shows every change and rollback.
- **Acceptance criteria:** A bad config change can be rolled back in one step and the prior behaviour returns; the history shows the change and the rollback as separate versioned events; a maker cannot approve their own high-impact flag.

## M01-FR-04 — Branch opening, temporary closure and permanent closure

- **Statement:** The system shall provide governed workflows to open a branch, temporarily close it, reopen it, and permanently close/retire it, each with the required preconditions and evidence.
- **Business reason:** Opening/closing a branch touches stock, cash, staff access and reporting; doing it ad-hoc leaks stock and access.
- **Actors & scope:** Owner (approve), Store Manager/Admin (execute), Finance/Inventory (verify balances).
- **Preconditions:** Branch exists (FR-01); for closure, open transactions and balances are known.
- **Main flow:** Open — configure branch, assign users/devices, confirm readiness, owner GO. Temporary close — set closure window, suspend trading, preserve state, reopen. Permanent close — settle/transfer stock and cash, revoke access, archive, owner sign-off.
- **Alternate / error flows:** Permanent closure blocked while stock, cash or open orders remain unresolved; temporary closure preserves reservations and unsent sync items.
- **Business rules:** A branch with non-zero stock/cash or open documents cannot be permanently closed until they are transferred or written off with approval; access is revoked on closure (joiner-mover-leaver, M02-FR-04); audit and data retention survive closure (§29.1, do-not rule "never delete audit evidence").
- **Permissions / approval:** Owner-approved; separation between the person executing and the person approving (§28).
- **Offline / sync (§31):** Closure is a cloud-governed action; the edge must be fully synced (zero unsent items) before permanent closure completes; conflicts block completion and become exceptions.
- **Audit / privacy / security:** Full immutable record; closure evidence pack retained.
- **Integration / reporting:** API-01/API-11; closed branches excluded from live trading reports but retained in history.
- **Acceptance criteria:** Attempting to permanently close a branch with unsold stock is blocked with the exact blocking reasons; after permanent closure all user access to that branch is revoked; audit history for the closed branch remains fully readable.
