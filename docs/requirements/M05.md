# M05 — Pricing and promotions

- **Priority:** P1 · **Release:** R2 · **Stage:** 7 · **Roadmap:** §5 M05, §16 D06, §30.2 (PriceActivated/PromotionActivated), §31
- **Purpose:** One price and promotion truth (P-02) that reaches POS, app and shelf correctly and **never sells below the margin floor or an unpublished/expired promotion** — including offline. Related extension: **D06** (best-price/stacking limits, markdown ladder, ESL price-integrity audit).

## M05-FR-01 — Price lists with effective dates
- **Shall:** manage store/zone/channel/customer price lists with effective dates and full change history.
- **Actors/scope:** Pricing user (maintain), Manager/Owner (approve). **Preconditions:** products (M03).
- **Flow:** create/edit price list → set effective date → approve → publish (signed pack to edge). **Alt/error:** overlapping effective prices resolved by explicit precedence; a future price does not activate early.
- **Rules:** prices are effective-dated; **in-flight sales keep the version they referenced** (§31.1); every price change is historised (append-only, §29.1); precedence: customer > channel > zone > store base.
- **Permissions (§28):** Pricing user maintains; change approved (maker-checker). **Offline (§31):** full from signed versioned local cache; retain last-known good.
- **Audit/privacy:** every price change audited who/from/to/when (M34); price-change history report. **Integration/reporting:** API-02; event `PriceActivated/RolledBack`.
- **Acceptance:** a scheduled price activates only on its effective date; a sale in progress is not repriced mid-transaction; price history shows who changed what.

## M05-FR-02 — MRP, cost, margin-floor and below-cost controls
- **Shall:** enforce MRP ceiling, and margin-floor / below-cost controls that block or require approval for loss-making prices.
- **Actors/scope:** Pricing user; approval by Manager/Owner. **Preconditions:** cost (M07 landed cost), MRP (M03).
- **Flow:** set price → system checks vs MRP and margin floor → block or route to approval if below floor/cost. **Alt/error:** below-floor price requires explicit approval with reason, never silent.
- **Rules:** selling price ≤ MRP; price ≥ margin floor unless approved; below-cost is an exception with approval + reason (P-03, P-08).
- **Permissions (§28):** below-floor/below-cost approval above the setter's authority. **Offline (§31):** floor/MRP checks run on the edge from cached cost/rules; deterministic best price offline.
- **Audit/privacy:** below-floor approvals audited. **Integration/reporting:** API-02; margin-exception report.
- **Acceptance:** a price above MRP is rejected; a below-margin-floor price is blocked pending approval and the approval is audited; the check also holds offline.

## M05-FR-03 — Promotions: BOGO, mix-match, multibuy, coupons, bank offers, member pricing
- **Shall:** support BOGO, mix-match, multibuy, bundles, coupons, bank offers and member pricing.
- **Actors/scope:** Pricing/Marketing user; consumed by POS/app. **Preconditions:** price lists (FR-01), customer/loyalty for member pricing (M16/M17).
- **Flow:** define promotion + eligibility + dates + funding → simulate (FR-04) → approve → publish to local promo pack. **Alt/error:** an unpublished or expired promotion never applies (§31 rule); conflicting promos resolved by stacking/exclusion rules.
- **Rules:** deterministic **best price** given the approved rule set; promotions effective-dated; member/bank offers validated against eligibility; **no expired/unpublished rule applies offline** (§31).
- **Permissions (§28):** promotion approved before publish. **Offline (§31):** rules present in the approved local pack; deterministic best price offline.
- **Audit/privacy:** promotion lifecycle audited; events `PromotionActivated/Stopped`. **Integration/reporting:** API-02; promotion-uptake report.
- **Acceptance:** the same basket yields the same best price online and offline; an expired promotion does not apply on an offline lane; stacking/exclusion rules are honoured deterministically.

## M05-FR-04 — Simulation, stacking/exclusions, vendor funding, effectiveness
- **Shall:** simulate promotions before launch, enforce stacking/exclusion and abuse limits, track vendor funding, and measure effectiveness.
- **Actors/scope:** Pricing/Marketing + Finance (funding). **Preconditions:** promotions (FR-03), sales data (M12).
- **Flow:** simulate impact on margin/volume → set stacking/exclusion + abuse caps (D06-FR-04) → record vendor funding → launch → measure. **Alt/error:** simulation showing margin breach warns before approval.
- **Rules:** abuse limits cap coupon/offer misuse; vendor funding tracked and reconciled to finance (M23); effectiveness compares vs baseline.
- **Permissions (§28):** Finance approves funding; abuse-limit override approved. **Offline (§31):** simulation online; caps enforced offline via the promo pack.
- **Audit/privacy:** funding and overrides audited. **Integration/reporting:** API-02/API-10; promotion-effectiveness and vendor-funding reports.
- **Acceptance:** a simulated promotion shows margin/volume impact before approval; a coupon cannot be used beyond its abuse limit; vendor-funded value reconciles to finance.

## Module acceptance & test families
- Deterministic best-price engine online **and** offline; margin-floor/MRP enforcement offline; effective-dated activation; no expired/unpublished promo applies.
- Audit: price/promotion change history complete; events emitted.
