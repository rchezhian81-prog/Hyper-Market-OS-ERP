# M06 — Supplier and procurement

- **Priority:** P1 · **Release:** R2 · **Stage:** 7 · **Roadmap:** §5 M06, §16 D03, §28 (SoD), §31
- **Purpose:** Buy well and safely — onboard suppliers with **bank-change verification** (a key fraud control), compare quotes, raise and approve POs, and track open commitments. Related extension: **D03** (MOQ/lead time, invoice OCR, rebates/schemes, VMI/consignment, PPV, supplier statement).

## M06-FR-01 — Supplier onboarding, documents, bank-change verification, risk status
- **Shall:** onboard suppliers with documents, KYC, **verified bank-account changes**, and a risk/blocked status.
- **Actors/scope:** Purchase user (request), Purchase Approver/Finance (approve supplier + bank). **Preconditions:** org (M01).
- **Flow:** capture supplier + documents → verify → approve → active; any **bank-detail change requires independent verification and approval** before payment. **Alt/error:** unverified bank change blocks payment; duplicate supplier flagged.
- **Rules:** **bank-change verification is mandatory** (D03-FR-06, fraud control M15); a user cannot both create a supplier and approve its bank details (§28); blocked suppliers cannot receive POs.
- **Permissions (§28):** maker ≠ approver for supplier/bank creation. **Offline (§31):** supplier master read from cache; onboarding/verification online.
- **Audit/privacy:** supplier and bank changes audited with before/after (M34); bank details classified/masked (PRV). **Integration/reporting:** API-03; supplier register and duplicate-bank-account report (M15-FR-03).
- **Acceptance:** a bank-detail change cannot take effect without independent verification; the creator of a supplier cannot approve its bank details; a blocked supplier cannot be POed.

## M06-FR-02 — Requisition, RFQ, quotation comparison, PO, blanket PO, approvals
- **Shall:** run requisition → RFQ → quotation comparison → PO (incl. blanket PO) with value-based approvals.
- **Actors/scope:** Purchase user (draft), Purchase Approver (approve). **Preconditions:** suppliers (FR-01), products (M03).
- **Flow:** requisition (demand/min-max/budget) → RFQ to suppliers → compare quotes → create PO → approve by value → issue. **Alt/error:** PO above limit escalates; quote comparison highlights price/lead-time differences.
- **Rules:** PO approval bound to value limits (M02-FR-03); quotation comparison is like-for-like; **a user cannot requisition, receive and pay the same transaction** (§28).
- **Permissions (§28):** approval by authority; separation of duties enforced. **Offline (§31):** PO drafting can cache where approved; issue/approval online (no unsafe stale approval, §31).
- **Audit/privacy:** RFQ/PO/approvals audited; event `PurchaseOrderApproved`. **Integration/reporting:** API-03; open-PO and price-comparison reports.
- **Acceptance:** a PO above a buyer's limit cannot be issued without the approver; quote comparison shows the cheapest/fastest clearly; SoD prevents one person completing requisition-to-pay.

## M06-FR-03 — Contracts, schemes, rebates, lead times, scorecards
- **Shall:** manage contracts, schemes/rebates, lead times and supplier scorecards.
- **Actors/scope:** Purchase + Finance (rebate claims). **Preconditions:** suppliers, POs.
- **Flow:** record contract/scheme/rebate + lead time → accrue rebate/funding claims (D03-FR-03) → score supplier on fill rate/price/quality. **Alt/error:** expiring contract flags for renewal.
- **Rules:** rebate/scheme accruals reconcile to finance (M23); scorecards use objective metrics (fill rate, price variance, lead time, quality).
- **Permissions (§28):** Finance approves rebate claims. **Offline (§31):** reference data read from cache; claims online.
- **Audit/privacy:** contract/rebate changes audited. **Integration/reporting:** API-03/API-10; supplier scorecard and rebate-accrual reports.
- **Acceptance:** a rebate accrues and reconciles to finance; a supplier scorecard reflects actual receipts; an expiring contract raises an alert.

## M06-FR-04 — Central/local procurement, amendments, cancellations, open commitments
- **Shall:** support central and local procurement, PO amendments/cancellations, and open-commitment tracking.
- **Actors/scope:** Purchase user/approver. **Preconditions:** POs (FR-02).
- **Flow:** raise central or local PO → amend/cancel with approval → track open commitment until received/closed. **Alt/error:** amendment after partial receipt adjusts the open commitment correctly.
- **Rules:** open commitment = ordered − received − cancelled; amendments/cancellations are approved and audited; central vs local scope respected (§28).
- **Permissions (§28):** amendment/cancellation approved. **Offline (§31):** commitments viewed from last sync; changes online.
- **Audit/privacy:** amendments/cancellations audited. **Integration/reporting:** API-03; open-commitment and PO-amendment reports.
- **Acceptance:** cancelling part of a PO reduces the open commitment correctly; an amended PO keeps a full history; open commitments reconcile to receipts.

## Module acceptance & test families
- SoD (§28/QG-06): requisition ≠ receive ≠ pay; supplier creator ≠ bank approver — proven by attempting the forbidden combination.
- Bank-change verification blocks payment until verified.
- Approvals bound to value limits; open-commitment math; rebate reconciliation.
