# M07 — Receiving, QC and three-way match

- **Priority:** P1 · **Release:** R2 · **Stage:** 7 · **Roadmap:** §5 M07, §16 D03/D05, §29 (Purchase/Inventory), §31
- **Purpose:** Turn a delivery into trusted stock and a matched, payable invoice — capturing batch/expiry/MRP/cost, catching discrepancies, and blocking payment on unmatched or variant invoices. This is where money leaks if the three-way match is weak.

## M07-FR-01 — ASN / direct-store-delivery, dock scheduling, receiving
- **Shall:** receive against a PO or ASN (and handle direct-store-delivery), with dock scheduling and handheld scanning.
- **Actors/scope:** Receiver/QC (handheld). **Preconditions:** PO (M06) or DSD policy; products (M03).
- **Flow:** book dock slot → scan PO/ASN → scan items (barcode/case) → capture quantities → create GRN. **Alt/error:** DSD without a PO follows a controlled receipt path with approval; unknown barcode blocked to a resolution queue.
- **Rules:** received quantity converts via UOM/case-pack (M03-FR-02); GRN references PO/ASN; **globally unique receiving command** so a re-scan doesn't double-count (§31.1).
- **Permissions (§28):** Receiver/QC only; **cannot change PO price**. **Offline (§31):** queue-capable — receiving works on the handheld offline; conflicts surfaced on sync, never last-write-wins.
- **Audit/privacy:** receipts audited; event `GoodsReceived`. **Integration/reporting:** API-03/API-04; receiving and dock reports.
- **Acceptance:** a case scan records the correct unit count; receiving completes fully offline and reconciles once on sync; a receiver cannot alter the PO price.

## M07-FR-02 — Count, batch, expiry, MRP, cost, damage, quality capture
- **Shall:** capture per-line count, batch/lot, expiry, MRP, cost, damage and quality status at receipt.
- **Actors/scope:** Receiver/QC. **Preconditions:** receiving started (FR-01).
- **Flow:** for each line capture qty + batch + expiry + MRP + cost + condition + QC status → hold failures. **Alt/error:** expired/near-expired or failed-QC stock routed to quarantine (FR-03); MRP mismatch vs master flagged.
- **Rules:** batch/expiry mandatory for batch-tracked items (M10); **cold-chain/temperature evidence** where required (D05-FR-04); captured cost feeds landed cost (FR-04) and valuation (M08).
- **Permissions (§28):** Receiver/QC. **Offline (§31):** full capture offline; queued.
- **Audit/privacy:** capture audited; QC evidence retained. **Integration/reporting:** API-04; goods-in-quality and expiry-at-receipt reports.
- **Acceptance:** a batch-tracked item cannot be received without batch + expiry; near-expiry stock is flagged at receipt; MRP different from master is flagged for review.

## M07-FR-03 — Short / excess / rejected receipt, quarantine, discrepancy approval
- **Shall:** handle short, excess and rejected receipts, quarantine, and discrepancy approval.
- **Actors/scope:** Receiver/QC (raise), Manager/Purchase (approve). **Preconditions:** capture (FR-02).
- **Flow:** detect short/excess/reject → quarantine affected stock → raise discrepancy → approve disposition (accept/return/claim). **Alt/error:** excess beyond tolerance requires approval; quarantined stock is not sellable/available.
- **Rules:** quarantine excludes stock from availability (M08 status); discrepancies are exceptions with owner/approver and value (P-03/P-08); supplier claim raised where due (M06/D03).
- **Permissions (§28):** discrepancy disposition approved above receiver authority. **Offline (§31):** quarantine/discrepancy captured offline; approval on sync.
- **Audit/privacy:** discrepancies and dispositions audited with value. **Integration/reporting:** API-03/API-04; discrepancy and quarantine reports.
- **Acceptance:** quarantined stock cannot be sold; an over-tolerance excess needs approval; every discrepancy carries a value and an owner.

## M07-FR-04 — PO-GRN-invoice matching, landed cost, evidence
- **Shall:** perform three-way PO-GRN-invoice matching with variance flags, compute landed cost, and attach evidence.
- **Actors/scope:** Purchase/Finance. **Preconditions:** PO (M06), GRN (FR-01), supplier invoice (M30/D03-FR-02 OCR ingestion).
- **Flow:** ingest invoice (incl. OCR/e-invoice) → match to PO + GRN → flag price/qty/tax variances → approve within tolerance → post to AP/Tally (M23). **Alt/error:** out-of-tolerance variance blocks auto-approval and routes to review; unmatched invoice cannot be paid.
- **Rules:** **no payment on an unmatched or out-of-tolerance invoice without approval**; landed cost includes freight/duty (D03-FR-05) and updates valuation (M08); variances valued and owned.
- **Permissions (§28):** variance approval above buyer; **receiver ≠ approver of payment** (§28). **Offline (§31):** matching is online/finance (§31 "no unsafe stale approval").
- **Audit/privacy:** match results, variances and approvals audited; event `SupplierInvoiceMatched/Exception`. **Integration/reporting:** API-03/API-09; three-way-match and price-variance reports.
- **Acceptance:** an invoice whose price exceeds tolerance vs PO is blocked from payment pending approval; landed cost reflects freight; an unmatched invoice cannot post to AP.

## Module acceptance & test families
- Offline receiving (§31/QG-04): full GRN with the network cable out; exactly-once on sync.
- Three-way match variance blocking; landed-cost calculation; quarantine excludes availability.
- SoD (§28): receiver cannot approve payment; discrepancy approval above receiver.
- **Priority (audit A-03):** prove bulk supplier-invoice import/OCR (M30 + D03-FR-02) against a real 80+ line invoice **first** in Stage 7.
