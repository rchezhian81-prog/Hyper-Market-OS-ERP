# M09 — Warehouse and replenishment

- **Priority:** P1/P2 · **Release:** R2 · **Stage:** 8 · **Roadmap:** §5 M09, §29, §31
- **Purpose:** Keep shelves and back-room full without over-buying — put-away, movement, min/max-driven replenishment, allocation, and honest physical counts. All movements write to the append-only ledger (M08).

## M09-FR-01 — Put-away, bin movement, pick/pack/dispatch, handheld scanning
- **Shall:** manage put-away, bin-to-bin movement, and internal pick/pack/dispatch via handheld scanning.
- **Actors/scope:** Warehouse user (handheld). **Preconditions:** receiving (M07), locations (M04-FR-02), ledger (M08).
- **Flow:** scan received stock → put away to bin → move/pick/dispatch as needed, each a scanned movement event. **Alt/error:** scan to a full/invalid bin blocked; unknown bin queued.
- **Rules:** every movement is a **globally unique command** appended to the ledger (§31.1); bin capacity respected (M04).
- **Permissions (§28):** Warehouse within location scope; **cannot self-approve material variance**. **Offline (§31):** queue-capable on handheld; conflicts surfaced on sync.
- **Audit/privacy:** movements audited via ledger events. **Integration/reporting:** API-04; put-away and movement reports.
- **Acceptance:** a bin move offline appends exactly one event on sync; putting away beyond bin capacity is blocked; every movement is traceable to a user and time.

## M09-FR-02 — Min/max, safety stock, reorder point, demand-based replenishment
- **Shall:** compute replenishment from min/max, safety stock and reorder point, with demand-based suggestions.
- **Actors/scope:** Purchase/Manager; feeds purchase planning (WF-03) and AI purchase agent (A02, advisory only). **Preconditions:** availability (M08), sales history.
- **Flow:** compute needs → propose requisition/replenishment task → human approves (A02 may *draft*, buyer approves — AI-NFR-12). **Alt/error:** suggestion for a discontinued/blocked item suppressed.
- **Rules:** parameters per product/store; **AI only recommends** reorder, never commits a purchase (hard rule #5); safety stock respects lead time (M06/D03).
- **Permissions (§28):** buyer approves resulting PO (M06). **Offline (§31):** parameters cached; computation online/edge; proposals reconcile.
- **Audit/privacy:** parameter changes and AI suggestions logged (accept/reject — AI outcome tracking). **Integration/reporting:** API-04; replenishment-need and stockout-risk reports.
- **Acceptance:** a below-reorder-point item generates a replenishment proposal; an AI reorder suggestion cannot become a PO without buyer approval; parameters drive the numbers.

## M09-FR-03 — Warehouse-to-store allocation and inter-store recommendations
- **Shall:** allocate warehouse stock to stores and recommend inter-store transfers.
- **Actors/scope:** Warehouse/Manager (approve transfer). **Preconditions:** multi-location stock (M08).
- **Flow:** compute allocation/transfer recommendation → approve → execute transfer (WF-07: request→approve→dispatch→in-transit→receive). **Alt/error:** transfer of quarantined/expired stock blocked.
- **Rules:** transfer creates in-transit state at source and adds on receipt at destination (M08-FR-02); recommendations are advisory until approved.
- **Permissions (§28):** transfer approved; receiver at destination confirms. **Offline (§31):** transfer steps queue-capable; discrepancy on receipt surfaced.
- **Audit/privacy:** transfers audited via ledger. **Integration/reporting:** API-04; allocation and transfer reports.
- **Acceptance:** a transfer shows in-transit until received; a receipt discrepancy raises an exception with value; quarantined stock cannot be transferred.

## M09-FR-04 — Cycle count, blind physical count, variance investigation
- **Shall:** run cycle and blind physical counts, with variance root-cause and financial impact.
- **Actors/scope:** Warehouse/Manager (count), Manager/Owner (approve adjustment). **Preconditions:** ledger (M08).
- **Flow:** plan count + freeze rules → **blind** count (no expected qty shown) → recount variances → assign root cause → approve adjustment (M08-FR-03). **Alt/error:** large variance escalates; unexplained variance stays an open exception.
- **Rules:** blind counting (counter cannot see system qty) to protect integrity; variance valued; adjustment is a compensating event with approval (§28); WF-08.
- **Permissions (§28):** counter ≠ sole approver of their variance. **Offline (§31):** counts captured offline; reconciled on sync.
- **Audit/privacy:** counts, variances, causes and approvals audited. **Integration/reporting:** API-04; count-variance and accuracy reports.
- **Acceptance:** the counter cannot see the expected quantity while counting; a variance produces a valued, reason-coded, approved adjustment; the aisle you count reconciles to the ledger or the difference is explained (roadmap Stage 8 owner check).

## Module acceptance & test families

- Offline (§31/QG-04): counts/movements queue and reconcile exactly once.
