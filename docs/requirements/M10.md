# M10 — Batch, expiry, quality and recall

- **Priority:** P1 · **Release:** R2 · **Stage:** 8 · **Roadmap:** §5 M10, §16 D05, §9.3 (FSSAI), §30.2, §31
- **Purpose:** Stop money leaking to expiry and protect customers — sell oldest-first, act on expiry early, keep cold-chain evidence, and be able to trace and recall a batch from supplier to the exact customers who bought it. Compliance validator: food-safety professional (§9.3, C-02).

## M10-FR-01 — FEFO allocation, expiry alerts, markdown and disposal
- **Shall:** allocate stock First-Expiry-First-Out, alert on approaching expiry, and drive markdown or disposal.
- **Actors/scope:** Inventory/Store staff; feeds pricing (M05 markdown) and waste (M28). **Preconditions:** batch/expiry captured (M07-FR-02), ledger (M08).
- **Flow:** allocate by earliest expiry → generate expiry action list (near-expiry) → propose markdown or route to disposal → confirm. **Alt/error:** expired stock auto-moves to expired state (not sellable, M08-FR-02).
- **Rules:** FEFO by default for batch-tracked items; **expiry action list produced first within Stage 8** (audit — measurable savings from day one); disposal is evidenced (M28).
- **Permissions (§28):** markdown per M05 authority; disposal approved. **Offline (§31):** expiry list and FEFO computed at edge from cached ledger.
- **Audit/privacy:** markdown/disposal decisions audited. **Integration/reporting:** API-04; **expiry action list** and wastage reports.
- **Acceptance:** the expiry list produced today matches what is actually on the shelf (owner check); sales allocate the earliest-expiry batch; expired stock cannot be sold.

## M10-FR-02 — Quality status, sampling, cold-chain and temperature evidence
- **Shall:** track quality status and sampling, and capture cold-chain/temperature evidence where required.
- **Actors/scope:** Receiver/QC, Store staff. **Preconditions:** products flagged for cold-chain/quality (M03) `⟳ AVR-12`.
- **Flow:** set quality status → record sampling results → capture temperature evidence at receipt/storage → hold/release. **Alt/error:** a cold-chain breach quarantines affected stock and raises an incident.
- **Rules:** cold-chain evidence retained (D05-FR-04, FSSAI); failed quality → quarantine (M07-FR-03); temperature excursions are exceptions (P-08).
- **Permissions (§28):** quality release by authorized QC. **Offline (§31):** evidence captured offline; queued.
- **Audit/privacy:** quality/temperature evidence retained (M34-FR-03). **Integration/reporting:** API-04; cold-chain and quality reports.
- **Acceptance:** a cold-chain breach quarantines the affected batch and records evidence; quality-held stock is not sellable until released; temperature evidence is retrievable for audit.

## M10-FR-03 — Supplier-to-customer lot traceability
- **Shall:** trace any lot/batch from the supplier and GRN through stock movements to the sales/customers that received it.
- **Actors/scope:** QC/Manager/Compliance. **Preconditions:** batch capture (M07), ledger (M08), sales with batch (M12), customer (M16).
- **Flow:** select a batch → show inbound (supplier/GRN) and outbound (sales, and identified customers where captured). **Alt/error:** where a sale had no identified customer, the sale/time is still traced.
- **Rules:** batch recorded at receipt and at sale; traceability works **backwards and forwards** (roadmap Stage 16 test "backwards"); privacy-respecting customer identification (M16/PRV).
- **Permissions (§28):** traceability read by authorized roles; customer PII minimized. **Offline (§31):** trace uses last-synced data with freshness shown.
- **Audit/privacy:** trace queries logged; customer data access controlled (PRV). **Integration/reporting:** API-04/API-06; lot-trace report.
- **Acceptance:** selecting a batch shows its supplier, GRN, movements and the sales/customers who bought it; the trace works from supplier→customer and customer→supplier.

## M10-FR-04 — Recall initiation, affected identification, closure evidence
- **Shall:** initiate a recall, identify affected stock and customers, block sale, and record closure evidence.
- **Actors/scope:** Compliance/Owner (initiate), Store/Service (execute customer contact). **Preconditions:** traceability (FR-03), recall-block flag (M03-FR-03).
- **Flow:** initiate recall on a batch → **recall-block stops POS sale and ordering** → identify affected stock (quarantine) and customers (notify per consent) → collect/dispose → record closure evidence (WF-09). **Alt/error:** attempting to sell a recalled batch is blocked everywhere, including offline.
- **Rules:** recall block is immediate and honoured offline (§31); affected customer contact respects consent (M16); closure requires evidence (P-04).
- **Permissions (§28):** recall initiated by authorized role; owner informed. **Offline (§31):** recall block propagates as priority sync; POS honours cached block.
- **Audit/privacy:** full recall record with evidence retained (never deleted — hard rule #6). **Integration/reporting:** API-04/API-06; recall status and closure report; event `BatchRecalled`.
- **Acceptance:** a recall test identifies the affected batch and the customers who bought it; a recalled batch cannot be sold even with the network cable out; the recall closes only with evidence.

## Module acceptance & test families
- FEFO allocation; expiry-list accuracy vs shelf; cold-chain breach → quarantine; **recall backwards-trace**; recall-block offline.
- Audit: recall and cold-chain evidence immutable and retained.
