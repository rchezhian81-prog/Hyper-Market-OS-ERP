# M14 — Till, cash office and day close

- **Priority:** P1 · **Release:** R2 · **Stage:** 9 · **Roadmap:** §5 M14, §16 D04, §29, §31
- **Purpose:** Control the cash and settle the day — float, pickups, safe drops, blind cash counts, tender settlement, and a locked day-close with an evidence pack your CA can trust. Cash operations must work offline.

## M14-FR-01 — Register/till assignment, float, loan, pickup, safe drop
- **Shall:** assign tills to cashiers and manage float issue, loans, pickups and safe drops.
- **Actors/scope:** Cash Office (issue), Cashier (custody). **Preconditions:** users (M02), store open (M01-FR-04).
- **Flow:** assign till + issue float → during trade record pickups/loans/safe drops → all as cash-movement events. **Alt/error:** issuing a till already assigned blocked; unrecorded variance surfaces at count.
- **Rules:** one custodian per till at a time; every cash movement is an event (append-only); pickups reduce till, add to safe (auditable chain).
- **Permissions (§28):** Cash Office issues; Cashier custodies; **cash office cannot alter a completed sale** (§28). **Offline (§31):** all cash movements full offline (§31 till/shift/close class).
- **Audit/privacy:** every cash movement audited; `CashMovement`. **Integration/reporting:** API-05; till and cash-movement reports.
- **Acceptance:** a till cannot be issued to two cashiers at once; a pickup moves value from till to safe with an audit trail; all cash movements work offline.

## M14-FR-02 — Cashier shift, blind count, denomination, over/short
- **Shall:** open/close cashier shifts with **blind** cash counts by denomination and over/short calculation.
- **Actors/scope:** Cashier + Cash Office. **Preconditions:** till assigned (FR-01).
- **Flow:** open shift → trade → **blind count** at close (system-expected hidden) → enter denominations → system computes over/short. **Alt/error:** large over/short escalates and is investigated (M15).
- **Rules:** blind count protects integrity (mirror of M09 blind count); over/short recorded and reason-coded; expected cash = float + cash sales − pickups − refunds.
- **Permissions (§28):** Cash Office reviews over/short. **Offline (§31):** shift and count fully offline; reconcile to cloud later.
- **Audit/privacy:** counts and over/short audited. **Integration/reporting:** API-05; over/short and cashier-accuracy reports.
- **Acceptance:** the cashier cannot see expected cash while counting; over/short computes correctly; a large short raises a loss-prevention exception.

## M14-FR-03 — Card/UPI/gateway settlement and exception investigation
- **Shall:** reconcile card/UPI/gateway settlements against POS tenders and investigate exceptions.
- **Actors/scope:** Cash Office/Finance. **Preconditions:** tenders (M12), provider statements (M23/§35).
- **Flow:** import/settle provider batch → match to POS tenders → flag unmatched/short settlements → investigate. **Alt/error:** a settlement mismatch is an exception with value; pending tenders reconciled (M12-FR-03).
- **Rules:** every electronic tender is independently reconcilable (§6.2); no card data used in reconciliation (tokens/refs only, hard rule #3).
- **Permissions (§28):** Finance/Cash Office. **Offline (§31):** settlement is online; POS tenders captured offline reconcile when settlement arrives.
- **Audit/privacy:** settlement matches and exceptions audited. **Integration/reporting:** API-05/API-09; settlement-reconciliation report.
- **Acceptance:** card settlements reconcile to POS card tenders; an unsettled/mismatched tender is flagged with value; reconciliation uses no card PAN.

## M14-FR-04 — Store/day close, locking, controlled reopen, evidence pack
- **Shall:** close the store/day with locking, controlled reopen, and a complete evidence pack, honouring the trading-day rule.
- **Actors/scope:** Store Manager/Cash Office. **Preconditions:** all lanes/shifts closed; trading day (M01-FR-02).
- **Flow:** close lanes → settle tenders → resolve exceptions → close day (locked) → produce evidence pack (sales, tenders, cash, over/short, exceptions). **Alt/error:** close blocked while unresolved exceptions/unsent sync remain; reopen is controlled and audited.
- **Rules:** **day close aligns to the trading-day cut-off** (M01-FR-02, A-13); a closed day is locked (append-only corrections only); reopen requires approval and is audited.
- **Permissions (§28):** close/reopen by Manager; reopen approved. **Offline (§31):** local close evidence offline; cloud reconciliation later; close completes only when reconciled.
- **Audit/privacy:** close/reopen and evidence pack audited/retained. **Integration/reporting:** API-05/API-09; day-close evidence pack feeds finance (M23) and owner (M29).
- **Acceptance:** the day cannot close with unresolved exceptions or unsent sales; a reopen is approved and audited; the evidence pack reconciles and a CA can sign it (feeds M23/QG-07).

## Module acceptance & test families
