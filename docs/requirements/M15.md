# M15 — Loss prevention and fraud

- **Priority:** P1/P2 · **Release:** R2 · **Stage:** 9 · **Roadmap:** §5 M15, §29, §31
- **Purpose:** Surface the risky patterns — suspicious voids, refunds, discounts, no-sales and cash anomalies, plus coupon/loyalty/supplier fraud — as **exceptions to the owner** (P-03), and manage cases with preserved evidence. This is "control by exception" made concrete.

## M15-FR-01 — Void, refund, discount, no-sale and cash-anomaly rules
- **Shall:** detect and alert on suspicious voids, refunds, discounts, no-sales and cash anomalies against configurable rules.
- **Actors/scope:** System (detect), Manager/Owner (act). **Preconditions:** POS/returns/cash data (M12–M14).
- **Flow:** transactions evaluated against rules/thresholds → anomaly raises an exception → alert to owner inbox (M29) / case (FR-04). **Alt/error:** false-positive feedback tunes rules; a spike escalates.
- **Rules:** rules are configurable per store/role; anomalies are **exceptions surfaced, not noise** (P-03); high void/refund/no-sale rates by cashier flagged.
- **Permissions (§28):** rule config by Security/Owner. **Offline (§31):** signals computed on synced data; offline actions (overrides) captured at POS feed detection on sync.
- **Audit/privacy:** anomalies and actions audited. **Integration/reporting:** API-05/API-10; loss-prevention exception feed → owner alerts (M29-FR-03).
- **Acceptance:** an unusual void/refund pattern raises a real alert to the owner; the alert links to the underlying transactions; rules are configurable without code.

## M15-FR-02 — Coupon, loyalty, delivery, supplier and purchase fraud signals
- **Shall:** detect fraud signals across coupons, loyalty, delivery, supplier and purchase activity.
- **Actors/scope:** System + Manager/Finance. **Preconditions:** promotions (M05), loyalty (M17), OMS/delivery (M18/M19), purchase (M06).
- **Flow:** evaluate signals (coupon abuse beyond limits, loyalty manipulation, delivery COD anomalies, supplier price/quantity anomalies) → raise exception/case. **Alt/error:** confirmed fraud feeds control tightening (e.g. abuse limits, D06-FR-04).
- **Rules:** cross-domain signals reconcile to their source; AI fraud agent (A07) **summarizes and prioritizes only — no autonomous sanctions** (§7 authority).
- **Permissions (§28):** investigation by authorized roles. **Offline (§31):** detection online/near-real-time on synced data.
- **Audit/privacy:** signals and outcomes audited; PII minimized in review (PRV). **Integration/reporting:** API-10; fraud-signal report.
- **Acceptance:** coupon use beyond its abuse limit raises a signal; a supplier invoice priced abnormally vs history is flagged (ties M06/M07); the AI agent cannot itself block or sanction.

## M15-FR-03 — Employee purchases, related-party, duplicate bank-account controls
- **Shall:** control employee purchases and related-party risk, and detect duplicate supplier/employee bank accounts.
- **Actors/scope:** System + Finance/HR/Owner. **Preconditions:** users (M02), suppliers (M06), payments (M23).
- **Flow:** flag employee purchases for policy; detect related-party links; detect duplicate bank accounts across suppliers/employees. **Alt/error:** duplicate bank account blocks payment until resolved (ties M06-FR-01).
- **Rules:** **duplicate bank-account detection** is a mandatory control (roadmap M15-FR-03, links D03-FR-06 bank-change verification); related-party transactions require disclosure/approval.
- **Permissions (§28):** sensitive; Finance/Owner scope; strict access to bank data (PRV). **Offline (§31):** online controls.
- **Audit/privacy:** flags and resolutions audited; bank data masked/classified. **Integration/reporting:** API-09/API-10; duplicate-bank-account and related-party reports.
- **Acceptance:** two suppliers sharing a bank account are flagged and payment is blocked pending review; employee purchases are visible to policy; related-party links are surfaced.

## M15-FR-04 — Case management, evidence preservation, outcome feedback
- **Shall:** manage investigation cases with preserved evidence and feed outcomes back to tighten controls.
- **Actors/scope:** Manager/Security/Owner. **Preconditions:** exceptions/signals (FR-01–03).
- **Flow:** open case from exception → gather/preserve evidence → investigate → record outcome → feed back to rules. **Alt/error:** evidence cannot be altered/deleted; case closure requires an outcome.
- **Rules:** **evidence is preserved and never deleted** (hard rule #6); outcomes tune detection (continuous improvement); chain of custody kept.
- **Permissions (§28):** case access restricted; audit-proof. **Offline (§31):** cases are cloud-managed (online).
- **Audit/privacy:** immutable case and evidence trail (M34). **Integration/reporting:** API-10/API-11; case and outcome reports.
- **Acceptance:** case evidence cannot be edited or deleted; a closed case has a recorded outcome; outcomes measurably adjust the rules that raised them.

## Module acceptance & test families
- Anomaly rules raise real, linked exceptions to the owner; duplicate-bank-account blocks payment; AI fraud agent cannot sanction (guardrail-aligned, AI-NFR-12); case evidence immutable (hard rule #6).
