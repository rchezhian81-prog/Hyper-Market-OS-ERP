# M16 — Customer 360 and consent

- **Priority:** P1 · **Release:** R4 · **Stage:** 14 (Customer commerce) · **Roadmap:** §5 M16, §16 D07, §9.2 (privacy), §28, §31, §35
- **Purpose:** One trustworthy view of the customer — guest, registered or household — with consent and privacy rights honoured, so every channel shares **one customer truth** (P-02) without misusing personal data (P-04).

## M16-FR-01 — Unified guest/registered identity, household, contact verification
- **Shall:** maintain one identity for a customer across guest, registered and household
  contexts, with contact verification and governed duplicate merge.
- **Actors/scope:** Customer (self-register), CRM/Service (manage), POS (link at sale).
  **Preconditions:** customer data model; `⟳ AVR-14` (app launch geography/policy).
- **Flow:** capture identity (OTP/contact verify) → detect duplicates → merge under
  governance → link household. **Alt/error:** an uncertain match is a **review exception**,
  not an auto-merge (P-08 / §31.1).
- **Rules:** one customer truth across channels (P-02); a merge is reversible and audited;
  a guest sale needs no PII (M12 guest fallback).
- **Permissions (§28):** merge/de-dupe governed; PII access least-privilege. **Offline
  (§31):** customer lookup cached/minimized; guest-sale fallback offline.
- **Audit/privacy:** identity changes audited; PII minimized (🔒). **Integration:** API-06.
- **Acceptance:** two records for one person merge under audit and can be reversed; a
  walk-in buys with no personal data; household links share entitlements per policy.

## M16-FR-02 — Consent purpose, channel preference, withdrawal and history
- **Shall:** record consent by purpose and channel, preferences, and a full withdrawal
  history, enforced everywhere marketing or profiling happens.
- **Actors/scope:** Customer (self), CRM (respect). **Preconditions:** identity (FR-01);
  `⟳ AVR-15` (consent/privacy practices).
- **Flow:** capture consent per purpose/channel → enforce at send time (M21) → a withdrawal
  takes effect immediately → history retained. **Alt/error:** a send that would breach
  consent/frequency is **blocked**, not warned-and-allowed.
- **Rules:** consent-scoped processing (PRV); frequency caps; withdrawal immediate and logged.
- **Permissions:** consent editable by the customer; staff changes audited. **Offline:**
  consent state cached read-only; sends are online.
- **Audit/privacy:** consent and withdrawals immutably logged (PRV). **Integration:** API-06.
- **Acceptance:** withdrawing consent stops the next campaign immediately; a channel with no
  consent is never used; the consent history is complete.

## M16-FR-03 — Access, correction, export, erasure / retention handling
- **Shall:** let a customer exercise data-subject rights — access, correction, export and
  erasure — within retention obligations.
- **Actors/scope:** Customer (raise), Admin/DPO (fulfil). **Preconditions:** identity;
  retention policy; `⟳ AVR-15`.
- **Flow:** raise the request in the privacy centre → verify → fulfil within the policy SLA
  → retain evidence. **Alt/error:** erasure conflicting with a legal-retention/financial
  record → the record is **retained per law and the customer is told what and why** (honest,
  P-08).
- **Rules:** rights honoured within retention limits (PRV); erasure never deletes audit or
  financial evidence required by law (#6 spirit).
- **Permissions:** self-service + verified staff; every fulfilment audited. **Offline:** online.
- **Audit/privacy:** every rights request and outcome logged. **Integration:** API-06.
- **Acceptance:** an export returns the customer's data; an erasure removes what law allows
  and clearly states what is retained and why; requests are self-service.

## M16-FR-04 — Segments, lifetime value, complaints, engagement history
- **Shall:** derive segments, CLV, complaint and engagement history for service and
  consent-safe marketing.
- **Actors/scope:** CRM/Owner (read). **Preconditions:** identity, orders, cases.
- **Flow:** compute segments/CLV from orders + loyalty + cases → available to CRM/BI under
  consent. **Alt/error:** profiling respects consent (FR-02).
- **Rules:** derived and consent-scoped; **no profiling without a lawful basis** (PRV).
- **Permissions:** least-privilege; branch scope. **Offline:** read-model/reporting,
  freshness shown.
- **Audit/privacy:** segment use audited. **Integration:** API-06 / API-10.
- **Acceptance:** CLV and segments compute against known data; a non-consented customer is
  excluded from marketing segments; complaint history is visible to service.

## Module acceptance & test families

  rights (access/export/erasure) within retention; profiling consent-scoped.
