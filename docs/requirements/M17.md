# M17 — Loyalty, membership and gift value

- **Priority:** P2 · **Release:** R4 · **Stage:** 14 (Customer commerce) · **Roadmap:** §5 M17, §16 D07, §29, §31, §35
- **Purpose:** Reward and retain customers with points, tiers, memberships and gift value that **reconcile exactly and never leak money**. Loyalty is a **liability on the books** (M23) — it must be trustworthy, fraud-resistant, and consistent across channels.

> Appendix B records; `⟳ AVR-##` marks store facts; §5 cross-cutting inherited. Related
> extension **D07**. Design surface: `../design/screens/customer-app.md`.

## M17-FR-01 — Earn/burn, tiers, expiry, reversal and liability
- **Shall:** manage points earn/burn, tiers, expiry, reversal, and the outstanding liability.
- **Actors/scope:** POS/app (earn/burn), Finance (liability). **Preconditions:** customer
  identity (M16); `⟳ AVR-14` (loyalty policy); rules configured.
- **Flow:** a sale earns points per rule → burn at tender (M12) → tier recalculated →
  expiry ages points → liability updated (M23). **Alt/error:** offline earn/burn follows
  **caps to prevent double-spend** (M12-FR-03); a reversal is a compensating entry.
- **Rules:** points are money-like — **every movement is an auditable event and the balance
  is projected from events**; expiry/reversal governed; liability reconciles (M23).
- **Permissions (§28):** rule changes and manual adjustments approved. **Offline (§31):**
  earn/burn within offline caps; reconciled on sync.
- **Audit/privacy:** every points movement audited. **Integration:** API-06 / API-09.
- **Acceptance:** points earned/burned reconcile to the liability; an offline burn cannot
  exceed the cap; expiry and reversal are auditable.

## M17-FR-02 — Coupons, referrals, memberships, subscriptions, personalized offers
- **Shall:** issue coupons, referrals, memberships and subscriptions, and personalized
  (consent-safe) offers.
- **Actors/scope:** CRM (issue), customer (redeem). **Preconditions:** identity + consent
  (M16); `⟳ AVR-14`.
- **Flow:** issue → validate at redemption (single-use, expiry, eligibility) → personalized
  offers under consent (M16-FR-02). **Alt/error:** invalid/expired/duplicate redemption
  blocked.
- **Rules:** single-use/expiry enforced; personalization consent-scoped (PRV); fraud limits
  (FR-03).
- **Permissions:** issuance governed; value limits approved. **Offline:** redemption
  validated online where value is at risk; low-value per policy offline.
- **Audit/privacy:** issuance/redemption audited. **Integration:** API-06.
- **Acceptance:** a coupon can't be redeemed twice or after expiry; a referral rewards only
  on a qualifying action; offers respect consent.

## M17-FR-03 — Gift cards, store credit, fraud limits, balance reconciliation
- **Shall:** manage gift cards and store credit with fraud limits and exact balance
  reconciliation.
- **Actors/scope:** POS/app, Finance. **Preconditions:** `⟳ AVR-14`; tender config (M12).
- **Flow:** issue/load → redeem at tender → balance updated → reconcile to liability (M23).
  **Alt/error:** over-redemption blocked; suspicious velocity flagged.
- **Rules:** gift/store-credit are liabilities; balances reconcile; fraud limits enforced;
  **no card data** (#3) — provider tokens where a card is involved.
- **Permissions:** issuance/limits approved. **Offline:** store-credit redemption within
  offline caps (double-spend prevention, M12).
- **Audit/privacy:** every load/redeem audited. **Integration:** API-06 / API-09.
- **Acceptance:** a gift-card balance never goes negative; store-credit offline can't be
  double-spent; balances reconcile to finance.

## M17-FR-04 — Family pooling and omnichannel consistency
- **Shall:** support family/household pooling of loyalty value and keep balances consistent
  across channels.
- **Actors/scope:** customer/household (M16), all channels. **Preconditions:** household
  identity (M16-FR-01).
- **Flow:** pool points/credit across a household per policy → one consistent balance online
  and in-store. **Alt/error:** concurrent burn across channels resolved **without
  double-spend** (§31.1 — conflict as exception, never last-write-wins).
- **Rules:** one balance truth across channels (P-02); pooling governed by household policy.
- **Permissions:** household changes governed. **Offline:** balance cached; burns reconcile;
  conflicts surface.
- **Audit/privacy:** pooling changes audited. **Integration:** API-06.
- **Acceptance:** a household shares one consistent balance; the same points can't be spent
  twice across two channels; pooling follows policy.

## Module acceptance & test families

  expiry; omnichannel one-balance consistency; fraud limits.
