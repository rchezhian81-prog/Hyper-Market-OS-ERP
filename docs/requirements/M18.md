# M18 — Order management and omnichannel

- **Priority:** P1/P2 · **Release:** R5 · **Stage:** 15 (Fulfilment) · **Roadmap:** §5 M18, §16 D09, §6.2 (omnichannel invariants), §31
- **Purpose:** One order lifecycle across every channel, with stock reserved so we **never oversell** (§6.2) and orders routed, split and reconciled honestly. The bridge between the customer app (M20) and fulfilment (M19).

## M18-FR-01 — Unified order lifecycle and branch/serviceability selection
- **Shall:** manage one order lifecycle across channels, selecting a serviceable
  branch/fulfilment location.
- **Actors/scope:** Customer (M20), OMS. **Preconditions:** catalogue/stock (M03/M08),
  serviceability; `⟳ AVR-13` (delivery radius/capacity/slots).
- **Flow:** order placed → serviceable branch/dark-store selected → lifecycle states
  (placed → confirmed → picking → packed → dispatched → delivered/collected). **Alt/error:**
  out-of-serviceability refused clearly (M20-FR-03); capacity full → alternative slot.
- **Rules:** one order truth across channels (P-02); serviceability configurable (10-km,
  D08); states auditable.
- **Permissions (§28):** order actions scoped. **Offline (§31):** order/promise online
  required; assigned fulfilment work cached (M19).
- **Audit/privacy:** lifecycle audited; PII minimized. **Integration:** API-07.
- **Acceptance:** an order routes to a serviceable location; an out-of-area order is
  refused; the lifecycle is visible end-to-end.

## M18-FR-02 — Stock reservation, order routing, split/partial order, backorder
- **Shall:** reserve stock, route orders, and support split/partial fulfilment and
  backorder policy — **without oversell**.
- **Actors/scope:** OMS, Inventory (M08). **Preconditions:** availability (M08-FR-02).
- **Flow:** on confirm, reserve stock (**excluded from walk-in availability**, M08) → route
  to branch(es) → split/partial per availability → backorder per policy. **Alt/error:**
  insufficient stock → substitution (FR-04) or backorder; a reservation conflict → exception
  (§31.1).
- **Rules:** **reserved stock is not sellable to a walk-in — no oversell** (§6.2,
  M08-FR-02); routing by capacity/proximity; backorder governed.
- **Permissions:** routing/backorder governed. **Offline:** reservation is edge-coordinated;
  POS respects reservations from cache.
- **Audit/privacy:** reservations audited. **Integration:** API-07 / API-04.
- **Acceptance:** reserving for an online order removes that stock from what a walk-in can
  buy; a partial order fulfils what's available and backorders the rest per policy; **no
  oversell under concurrency**.

## M18-FR-03 — Pickup, scheduled/express delivery, dark-store readiness
- **Shall:** support pickup, scheduled and express delivery, and dark-store fulfilment.
- **Actors/scope:** Customer, OMS, fulfilment (M19). **Preconditions:** slots/capacity;
  `⟳ AVR-13`.
- **Flow:** choose pickup or delivery (scheduled/express) → capacity-checked slot →
  fulfilment assigned (M19). **Alt/error:** slot/capacity full → alternatives; express
  unavailable → scheduled.
- **Rules:** capacity-aware slotting; dark-store as a fulfilment location; contribution-aware
  (D09 stop rules feed M19/finance).
- **Permissions:** scoped. **Offline:** promise online; assigned work offline (M19).
- **Audit/privacy:** fulfilment choice audited. **Integration:** API-07 / API-08.
- **Acceptance:** a pickup and a delivery order each route correctly; a full slot offers an
  alternative; a dark-store fulfils like a branch.

## M18-FR-04 — Cancellation, substitution, returns, refunds, channel reconciliation
- **Shall:** handle cancellation, controlled substitution, returns, refunds, and reconcile
  across channels.
- **Actors/scope:** Customer/Service (M21), OMS, Finance (M23). **Preconditions:** order;
  reservation.
- **Flow:** cancel (releases reservation) → substitution offered and **customer-confirmed**
  (A04) → returns/refunds via finance (M23) → channel reconciliation. **Alt/error:** a refund
  uncertain → pending, reconciled (**no fake refund**).
- **Rules:** **substitution requires customer confirmation** (A04, #5 spirit); refunds via
  tokens (**no card data** #3); every order reconciles across channels (§6.2).
- **Permissions (§28):** refund approval per policy. **Offline:** cancellation/refund online.
- **Audit/privacy:** cancellations/refunds/substitutions audited. **Integration:** API-07 /
  API-09.
- **Acceptance:** a cancellation releases reserved stock; a substitution never applies
  without customer confirmation; a refund uses a token and reconciles; channel totals tie.

## Module acceptance & test families
- No oversell under concurrency (reservation excludes availability); serviceability refusal;
  split/partial/backorder; customer-confirmed substitution; refund reconciliation.
