# M19 — Picking, packing and delivery

- **Priority:** P2 · **Release:** R5 · **Stage:** 15 (Fulfilment) · **Roadmap:** §5 M19, §16 D09, §31, §35
- **Purpose:** Turn a confirmed order into an accurate, well-handled delivery — picked, quality-checked, packed to temperature, and delivered with **honest proof and COD** — all on offline-capable handhelds, with location/PII minimized.

> extension **D09**. Design surfaces: `../design/screens/picker-packer.md`,
> `../design/screens/delivery.md`. Workflows **WF-14** (fulfilment), **WF-15** (delivery). API-08.

## M19-FR-01 — Wave/single picking, substitution preference, quality checks
- **Shall:** pick by wave or single order with substitution preference and quality checks.
- **Actors/scope:** Picker. **Preconditions:** assigned orders (M18), stock (M08); handheld.
- **Flow:** assigned wave → scan bin → scan item → confirm; a short-pick → **controlled
  substitution per the customer's preference/confirmation** (A04); quality check.
  **Alt/error:** item unavailable and no accepted substitute → line short, order updated (M18).
- **Rules:** every pick is a scan; **substitution honours the customer rule** (A04, #5
  spirit); weighed items capture final price (FR-02).
- **Permissions (§28):** picker scope. **Offline (§31):** **assigned work cached offline**;
  scans queue; **location/PII minimized**.
- **Audit/privacy:** picks/substitutions audited. **Integration:** API-08 / API-04.
- **Acceptance:** a picker completes a wave offline; a substitution follows the customer
  rule; a short-pick updates the order honestly.

## M19-FR-02 — Packing, temperature/handling requirements, dispatch
- **Shall:** pack orders with temperature/handling requirements and dispatch with a manifest.
- **Actors/scope:** Packer. **Preconditions:** picked order.
- **Flow:** weigh/finalize price (variable-weight, D09) → quality release → pack with
  **cold-chain/tamper evidence** → dispatch manifest. **Alt/error:** a cold-chain breach →
  quality hold.
- **Rules:** **weighted final price** captured (D09); cold-chain/tamper evidence; the
  manifest matches contents.
- **Permissions:** packer scope. **Offline:** queue-capable; evidence cached.
- **Audit/privacy:** packing/temperature audited. **Integration:** API-08.
- **Acceptance:** a weighed order's final price is captured; cold-chain evidence recorded;
  the manifest matches what was packed.

## M19-FR-03 — Driver/partner assignment, route, geofence, proof of delivery
- **Shall:** assign own drivers or delivery partners, plan routes with geofence, and capture
  proof of delivery.
- **Actors/scope:** Dispatcher, Driver/Partner. **Preconditions:** dispatched order;
  `⟳ AVR-13` (fleet/partner).
- **Flow:** assign driver/partner → route with geofence → deliver → **proof
  (photo/OTP/signature)**. **Alt/error:** partner unavailable → reassign; geofence mismatch
  flagged.
- **Rules:** proof mandatory per policy; **location/PII minimized** on device (§31/§35);
  partner access isolated (M24 readiness).
- **Permissions (§28):** assignment governed. **Offline (§31):** **assigned stops cached**;
  proof queues.
- **Audit/privacy:** assignment and proof audited; minimal PII. **Integration:** API-08.
- **Acceptance:** a stop captures proof offline; a reassignment is audited; location data is
  minimized.

## M19-FR-04 — COD, failed delivery, return-to-origin, SLA management
- **Shall:** handle COD collection, failed delivery, return-to-origin (RTO) and SLA management.
- **Actors/scope:** Driver, Dispatcher, Finance (M23). **Preconditions:** delivery in progress.
- **Flow:** collect **COD to the paisa** → on failure, reason → reattempt or RTO → SLA
  tracked → COD settled at shift end (feeds M23 reconciliation). **Alt/error:** COD mismatch
  → exception; **contribution stop rules** flag unprofitable patterns (D09).
- **Rules:** COD reconciles (**no card data** #3 — cash/UPI only); RTO governed;
  **contribution stop rules** surfaced (D09); SLA measured.
- **Permissions:** settlement governed. **Offline (§31):** COD/proof queue; reconcile on sync.
- **Audit/privacy:** COD/failures/RTO audited. **Integration:** API-08 / API-09.
- **Acceptance:** COD reconciles at shift end; a failed delivery records a reason and routes
  to reattempt/RTO; a contribution-stop condition is visible; SLA is measured.
