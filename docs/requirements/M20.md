# M20 — Customer mobile app and web commerce

- **Priority:** P2 · **Release:** R4 · **Stage:** 14 (Customer commerce) · **Roadmap:** §5 M20, §16 D08, §32, §31, §35, §10 (accessibility)
- **Purpose:** The public front door — an Android/iOS app and web store where a first-time shopper on a low-bandwidth phone can find, order and track goods in a few taps, with **honest availability** and privacy. **The customer always confirms the cart** (A04).

> Appendix B records; `⟳ AVR-##` marks store facts; §5 cross-cutting inherited. Related
> extension **D08**. Design surface: `../design/screens/customer-app.md`. Public surface —
> **WCAG 2.2 AA** (NFR-07). Workflow **WF-13** (customer order).

## M20-FR-01 — OTP/account, catalogue, search, filters, barcode/voice, multilingual UX
- **Shall:** provide OTP/account access and a catalogue with search, filters, barcode/voice
  input and English/Tamil UX.
- **Actors/scope:** Customer. **Preconditions:** catalogue published (M03/M05);
  `⟳ AVR-14` (launch geography/payments), `⟳ AVR-19` (languages/accessibility).
- **Flow:** OTP login (or guest browse) → search/filter/barcode/voice → product detail with
  content (D01) → add to cart. **Alt/error:** typo search → typo-tolerant results (D01);
  out-of-area handled at checkout (FR-03).
- **Rules:** catalogue reflects one commerce truth (P-02); English/Tamil; secure sessions (§35).
- **Permissions:** customer-scoped, least data. **Offline (§31):** browsing may cache;
  **order/payment require online** (clear message).
- **Audit/privacy:** session security; minimal PII (🔒). **Integration:** API-06 / API-02.
- **Acceptance:** a first-time user searches and adds to cart in a few taps; barcode/voice
  search work; the UI switches English/Tamil.

## M20-FR-02 — Cart, lists, favourites, repeat order, recommendations
- **Shall:** support cart, saved lists, favourites, one-tap repeat order, and consent-safe
  recommendations.
- **Actors/scope:** Customer. **Preconditions:** identity (M16) for saved lists; catalogue.
- **Flow:** build cart → save lists/favourites → repeat a past order → recommendations under
  consent (M16). **Alt/error:** an unavailable item offers a controlled
  substitution/alternative — **the customer confirms** (A04, D09).
- **Rules:** **the customer confirms the cart and any substitution** (A04, hard rule #5
  spirit — AI suggests only); recommendations consent-scoped.
- **Permissions:** customer-scoped. **Offline:** cart may cache; promise/price confirmed online.
- **Audit/privacy:** recommendation consent respected. **Integration:** API-06.
- **Acceptance:** a past basket reorders in a few taps; a substitution never applies without
  customer confirmation; recommendations respect consent.

## M20-FR-03 — Slots, payments, tracking, notifications, digital receipts, support
- **Shall:** offer delivery/pickup slots, online payment, order tracking, notifications,
  digital receipts and support.
- **Actors/scope:** Customer; payment via an **RBI-authorized provider** (§35).
  **Preconditions:** serviceability (D08); `⟳ AVR-13` (delivery radius/slots/fleet),
  `⟳ AVR-14` (payments).
- **Flow:** choose slot → see fees/minimum order → pay online (**provider tokens, no card
  data** #3) → track order → receive digital receipt/notifications → raise support.
  **Alt/error:** payment uncertain → **order not confirmed** (no fake approval, §31/§4.3);
  slot full → alternatives.
- **Rules:** **no card data** (#3); fees/minimum shown before pay; promise/authorization
  online required (§31); **10-km configurable serviceability** (D08).
- **Permissions:** customer-scoped. **Offline:** checkout/payment online; tracking cached
  read-only.
- **Audit/privacy:** payment tokenized; order/PII minimized. **Integration:** API-06 /
  API-07 (OMS), API-05 (finance tenders).
- **Acceptance:** a customer pays via a provider token (**no card number stored**); an
  out-of-area order is refused clearly; tracking and the digital receipt work.

## M20-FR-04 — Ratings, accessibility, privacy centre, account controls
- **Shall:** provide ratings, accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA), a privacy centre and account controls.
- **Actors/scope:** Customer. **Preconditions:** identity/consent (M16); `⟳ AVR-15`
  (privacy), `⟳ AVR-19` (accessibility).
- **Flow:** rate items/service → manage consent/rights in the privacy centre
  (M16-FR-02/03) → account controls (addresses, payment tokens, delete account).
  **Alt/error:** account deletion honours retention (M16-FR-03).
- **Rules:** **WCAG 2.2 AA** (NFR-07); privacy centre self-service (PRV); **low-bandwidth**
  support (D08).
- **Permissions:** customer-scoped. **Offline:** privacy actions online; content accessible.
- **Audit/privacy:** rights actions logged. **Integration:** API-06.
- **Acceptance:** the app meets WCAG 2.2 AA on key journeys; a customer manages consent and
  raises an erasure without contacting staff; it works on a low-spec phone at low bandwidth.

## Module acceptance & test families
- Search-to-cart in a few taps; customer-confirmed substitution (A04); **no card data**
  (guardrail); serviceability refusal; WCAG 2.2 AA; low-bandwidth performance.
