# M21 — CRM, marketing and service desk

- **Priority:** P2 · **Release:** R4 · **Stage:** 14 (Customer commerce) · **Roadmap:** §5 M21, §16 D07, §9.2 (consent/privacy), §28
- **Purpose:** Reach and serve customers **without ever breaching consent** — consent-safe campaigns, and a service desk that tracks every complaint to resolution with an SLA, where any **compensation is approved, not a free hand**.

> Appendix B records; `⟳ AVR-##` marks store facts; §5 cross-cutting inherited. Related
> extension **D07**. Design surface: `../design/screens/crm-service.md`. Workflows **WF-16**
> (marketing), **WF-17** (customer service).

## M21-FR-01 — Campaign segments and consent-safe WhatsApp/SMS/email/push
- **Shall:** build campaign segments and send across WhatsApp/SMS/email/push **only within
  consent and frequency rules**.
- **Actors/scope:** CRM/Marketing. **Preconditions:** segments + consent (M16); provider
  connectors (M32); `⟳ AVR-14` (marketing/service policy).
- **Flow:** build segment → the reachable count is computed **after consent filtering** →
  schedule/send within frequency caps → track delivery status. **Alt/error:** a send
  breaching consent/frequency is **blocked** (not warned-and-allowed).
- **Rules:** consent-safe by construction (PRV); frequency caps; provider policy (e.g.
  WhatsApp templates).
- **Permissions (§28):** campaign approval; spend/scale governed. **Offline:** online.
- **Audit/privacy:** every send audited against consent state. **Integration:** API-06; M31
  (messaging routing).
- **Acceptance:** a campaign cannot include a non-consented customer or breach a frequency
  cap; delivery status is visible; a consent withdrawal removes a customer before send.

## M21-FR-02 — Abandoned cart, occasions, win-back, attribution
- **Shall:** run abandoned-cart, occasion and win-back journeys and measure attribution.
- **Actors/scope:** CRM. **Preconditions:** consent (M16), orders (M18/M20).
- **Flow:** trigger journeys on events (cart abandoned, occasion, lapse) under consent →
  attribute outcomes to campaigns. **Alt/error:** journeys respect consent/frequency (FR-01).
- **Rules:** consent-scoped triggers; attribution derived.
- **Permissions:** governed. **Offline:** online.
- **Audit/privacy:** journey membership audited. **Integration:** API-06 / API-10.
- **Acceptance:** an abandoned-cart nudge goes only to a consented customer; attribution
  ties revenue to a campaign; frequency caps hold across journeys.

## M21-FR-03 — Complaint, enquiry, warranty, lost-and-found, compensation approval
- **Shall:** manage complaints, enquiries, warranty, lost-and-found and compensation with
  approval.
- **Actors/scope:** Service desk; Manager/Owner (approve compensation). **Preconditions:**
  identity (M16); `⟳ AVR-14` (service policy).
- **Flow:** raise case → triage/assign → resolve → **compensation (goodwill credit/refund)
  requires approval** (§28) → close with outcome. **Alt/error:** over-threshold compensation
  escalates; **AI may draft a reply but a human commits** (hard rule #5).
- **Rules:** **compensation is a financial action → maker-checker** (§28, #5); warranty per
  policy; every case auditable.
- **Permissions (§28):** compensation approval above agent authority. **Offline:** service
  is online; case-history freshness shown.
- **Audit/privacy:** cases and compensation audited; PII minimized. **Integration:** API-06;
  M23 (credit/refund), M17 (goodwill points).
- **Acceptance:** a compensation needs a separate approver; a complaint follows SLA; an
  AI-drafted reply is not sent until a human approves.

## M21-FR-04 — SLA, escalation, resolution and satisfaction
- **Shall:** enforce SLAs, escalation, resolution tracking and satisfaction measurement.
- **Actors/scope:** Service desk/Manager. **Preconditions:** cases (FR-03).
- **Flow:** SLA clock per case → escalate on breach → record resolution → measure
  satisfaction (CSAT). **Alt/error:** a breached SLA escalates visibly (P-03 / P-08).
- **Rules:** SLA/escalation policy; satisfaction captured.
- **Permissions:** scope by role. **Offline:** online.
- **Audit/privacy:** SLA and outcomes audited. **Integration:** API-06 / API-10.
- **Acceptance:** an SLA breach escalates and is visible; resolution and CSAT are recorded;
  a report shows service performance.
