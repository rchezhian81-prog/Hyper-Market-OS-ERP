# M22 — B2B and institutional sales

- **Priority:** P3 · **Release:** R6 · **Roadmap:** §5 M22, §16 D07/D10, §28, §29. **API-06 / API-09.**
- **Purpose:** Sell to **businesses and institutions**, not just walk-in customers — customer-specific pricing/credit/terms and contracts, the quote → order → proforma → challan → invoice workflow, bulk/recurring orders with salesperson and commission, and a **B2B portal** with outstanding and collection tracking. A second commercial channel on the same one-commerce-truth core (P-02).

> Appendix B records; `⟳ AVR-##` marks store facts; §5 cross-cutting inherited. Uses the
> exact-money primitives (`packages/pricing`, `packages/contracts`) and maker-checker
> (`packages/approvals`) already built.

## M22-FR-01 — Customer-specific price, credit, terms and contract
- **Shall:** hold per-B2B-customer price lists, credit limits, payment terms and contracts.
- **Actors/scope:** Sales/Finance. **Preconditions:** customer (M16), catalogue/price (M03/M05).
- **Flow:** define a B2B customer → assign a contract price list, credit limit and terms →
  orders price against it. **Alt/error:** an order exceeding the credit limit is **blocked
  pending approval** (§28); an expired contract falls back or blocks per policy.
- **Rules:** price/credit/terms are per-customer configuration (P-02 one truth); credit-limit
  breaches are exceptions, not silent overrides.
- **Permissions (§28):** contract/credit changes approved; separation from order-taking.
  **Offline (§31):** B2B pricing/credit is online (no unsafe stale credit).
- **Audit/privacy:** contract and credit changes audited (M34). **Integration:** API-06.
- **Acceptance:** a B2B order prices at the contract price; an over-limit order is blocked
  until approved; an expired contract is handled per policy.

## M22-FR-02 — Quote → sales order → proforma → challan → invoice
- **Shall:** run the full B2B document workflow: quotation, sales order, proforma invoice,
  delivery challan and tax invoice.
- **Actors/scope:** Sales/Finance. **Preconditions:** customer + pricing (FR-01); number
  series (M01-FR-02).
- **Flow:** quote → convert to sales order → proforma → challan on dispatch → tax invoice.
  **Alt/error:** a quote is non-committing until converted; partial delivery produces a
  partial challan/invoice.
- **Rules:** documents use **gap-free number series** (`packages/numbering`); money is exact
  (`packages/pricing`); GST correct (M23).
- **Permissions:** per role/value. **Offline (§31):** B2B documents are online; drafts may cache.
- **Audit/privacy:** each document versioned/audited. **Integration:** API-06/API-09.
- **Acceptance:** a quote converts to an order and flows to a correct tax invoice; numbers
  are gap-free; GST ties out.

## M22-FR-03 — Bulk / recurring orders, salesperson and commission
- **Shall:** support bulk and recurring orders, salesperson assignment and commission.
- **Actors/scope:** Sales/Finance. **Preconditions:** orders (FR-02).
- **Flow:** place a bulk order or a recurring schedule → attribute to a salesperson →
  compute commission. **Alt/error:** a recurring order with insufficient stock/credit raises
  an exception.
- **Rules:** commission computed with exact money (`packages/pricing`); recurring schedules
  are governed; no oversell (M08/M18).
- **Permissions:** commission rules approved. **Offline:** online.
- **Audit/privacy:** commission and recurring runs audited. **Integration:** API-06/API-09.
- **Acceptance:** a recurring order generates on schedule; commission computes exactly;
  bulk orders respect stock/credit.

## M22-FR-04 — B2B portal, outstanding and collection tracking
- **Shall:** give B2B customers a portal to order, see statements/outstanding, and support
  collections.
- **Actors/scope:** B2B customer (self-service), Finance. **Preconditions:** customer
  identity/consent (M16), AR (M23).
- **Flow:** customer logs in → places orders, views invoices/outstanding, pays → collections
  tracked (aging, reminders). **Alt/error:** overdue accounts flagged; portal access scoped
  to the customer's own data (**tenant + customer isolation**, ADR-0003).
- **Rules:** portal shows only the customer's own data; outstanding reconciles to AR (M23);
  no card data (#3).
- **Permissions:** customer-scoped; staff by role. **Offline:** portal online.
- **Audit/privacy:** portal access and payments audited; PII minimized. **Integration:**
  API-06/API-09.
- **Acceptance:** a B2B customer sees only its own invoices/outstanding; a payment reduces
  the outstanding and reconciles to finance; overdue accounts are flagged.
