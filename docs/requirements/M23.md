# M23 — Finance, tax and accounting bridge

- **Priority:** P1 · **Release:** R2 · **Stage:** 10 · **Roadmap:** §5 M23, §16 D10, §9.3 (GST), §30.2, §31
- **Purpose:** Make the money reconcile and the tax stand up — ledger mapping, GST, reconciliations, and a reliable Tally bridge. **Everything must reconcile and a CA (not a programmer) must be able to sign the control totals** (QG-07). Compliance validator: CA/tax professional (C-01).

## M23-FR-01 — Ledger mapping, journals, AP/AR, cost/profit centres
- **Shall:** map operational events to accounting ledgers, post journals, manage payable/receivable, and support cost/profit centres.
- **Actors/scope:** Finance. **Preconditions:** operational events (POS/purchase/inventory), chart of accounts `⟳ AVR-09`.
- **Flow:** define ledger mapping → operational events post journals → AP/AR tracked → cost/profit centre allocation. **Alt/error:** an unmapped event raises an exception (never silently unposted, P-08).
- **Rules:** posting is deterministic from mapping; **finance cannot edit the immutable operational ledger** (§28) — it reads and posts; corrections are journals.
- **Permissions (§28):** Finance posts/reconciles; segregation from operational data creation. **Offline (§31):** finance is generally online; POS/purchase feed on sync.
- **Audit/privacy:** postings audited. **Integration/reporting:** API-09; trial balance and AP/AR reports.
- **Acceptance:** every operational event maps to a journal or an exception; AP/AR reconciles to purchase/sales; cost-centre reports tie to the ledger.

## M23-FR-02 — GST configuration, credit/debit notes, tax audit evidence
- **Shall:** configure GST, issue credit/debit notes, and produce tax audit evidence and returns-supporting reports.
- **Actors/scope:** Finance + CA. **Preconditions:** tax classes (M03), sales/purchase.
- **Flow:** configure GST rules → transactions compute tax → credit/debit notes issued → generate returns-supporting reports and evidence. **Alt/error:** tax mismatch flagged; GST period aligns to the trading-day rule (M01/A-13).
- **Rules:** tax computed per HSN/tax class; **GST periods derive from the trading-day rule**; evidence sufficient for audit (C-01).
- **Permissions (§28):** GST config with CA validation. **Offline (§31):** tax computed at POS from cached rules; filing online.
- **Audit/privacy:** tax config and notes audited. **Integration/reporting:** API-09; GST and tax-audit reports.
- **Acceptance:** GST on a sample basket matches expectation; a credit note reverses tax correctly; returns-supporting reports reconcile to sales/purchase for the period.

## M23-FR-03 — Cash/bank/card/UPI/gateway/refund reconciliation
- **Shall:** reconcile cash, bank, card, UPI, payment-gateway and refund flows against POS and finance records.
- **Actors/scope:** Finance/Cash office. **Preconditions:** tenders (M12), settlements (M14), refunds (M13).
- **Flow:** import bank/gateway statements → match to POS tenders and refunds → flag exceptions with value. **Alt/error:** unmatched line is an exception, resolved same day (§34.1 parallel-run discipline).
- **Rules:** every tender/refund independently reconcilable (§6.2); **no card PAN used** — tokens/refs only (hard rule #3).
- **Permissions (§28):** Finance. **Offline (§31):** POS tenders captured offline reconcile when settlement arrives.
- **Audit/privacy:** reconciliation and exceptions audited; `ReconciliationExceptionRaised/Resolved` (§30.2). **Integration/reporting:** API-09; reconciliation dashboard.
- **Acceptance:** card/UPI settlements reconcile to POS tenders; a mismatch is an owned, valued exception; reconciliation uses no card number.

## M23-FR-04 — Tally connector, retry queue, period close, balance validation
- **Shall:** bridge to Tally via a versioned connector with a retry queue and dead-letter, and validate balances at period close.
- **Actors/scope:** Finance. **Preconditions:** ledger mapping (FR-01), Tally structure `⟳ AVR-09`.
- **Flow:** post to Tally through the connector → retry on failure → dead-letter poison items (visible) → validate control totals → close period. **Alt/error:** a dead-lettered posting stays visible and is reconciled, never dropped (§4.2).
- **Rules:** connector is idempotent and versioned (M32); **control totals must validate before period close** (QG-07); period close aligns to trading day; a closed period reopens only with approval (append-only corrections).
- **Permissions (§28):** period close/reopen by Finance with approval. **Offline (§31):** connector online; queue durable.
- **Audit/privacy:** postings, retries, dead-letters, close/reopen audited; `PeriodClosed/Reopened`. **Integration/reporting:** API-09; Tally-queue and control-total reports.
- **Acceptance:** a failed Tally posting retries and, if it fails, is visible in a dead-letter queue (not lost); period cannot close with unvalidated control totals; a CA can sign the control totals.

## Module acceptance & test families
- Reconciliation completeness (every tender/refund matched or an exception); GST correctness; Tally idempotent posting + visible dead-letter; period-close balance validation; **CA-signable control totals** (QG-07).
