# M24 — Supplier and external partner portals

- **Priority:** P3 · **Release:** R6 (extends the R2 purchase core) · **Roadmap:** §5 M24, §16 D14, §28, §35. **API-03 / API-11.**
- **Purpose:** Let suppliers and partners self-serve — respond to RFQs, submit catalogues/ASNs/invoices, acknowledge POs and see delivery/claims/statements — behind strict access isolation. A per-tenant **optional** feature (ADR-0003 entitlement).

> Appendix B records; §5 cross-cutting inherited. Reuses purchase (M06/M07), approvals and
> the versioned API/connector surface (M32).

## M24-FR-01 — RFQ response, catalogue/cost proposals, ASN and invoice submission
- **Shall:** let a supplier respond to RFQs, submit catalogue/cost proposals, ASNs and invoices.
- **Actors/scope:** Supplier (self-service), Buyer. **Preconditions:** supplier onboarded
  (M06); RFQ (M07).
- **Flow:** supplier logs in → responds to RFQ / uploads catalogue / sends ASN / submits
  invoice → routed into purchase (M07). **Alt/error:** malformed submissions rejected with
  reasons; nothing commits without buyer acceptance.
- **Rules:** submissions are drafts until accepted (§28); invoice submission feeds three-way
  match (M07-FR-04); no bank change via portal without verification (M06-FR-01).
- **Permissions:** supplier sees only its own data (**tenant + supplier isolation**, §35).
  **Offline:** portal online.
- **Audit/privacy:** submissions audited; partner PII minimized. **Integration:** API-03.
- **Acceptance:** a supplier submits an ASN/invoice that flows into receiving/match; a
  supplier cannot see another supplier's data.

## M24-FR-02 — PO acknowledgement, delivery status, claims and statement
- **Shall:** let a supplier acknowledge POs, update delivery status, raise claims and view statements.
- **Actors/scope:** Supplier, Buyer/Finance. **Preconditions:** PO (M06).
- **Flow:** PO issued → supplier acknowledges → delivery status updates → claims raised →
  statement/reconciliation viewable. **Alt/error:** disputed claim becomes an exception.
- **Rules:** statements reconcile to AP (M23); claims governed.
- **Permissions:** supplier-scoped. **Offline:** online.
- **Audit/privacy:** acknowledgements/claims audited. **Integration:** API-03/API-09.
- **Acceptance:** a PO acknowledgement and delivery update are visible to the buyer; a
  supplier statement ties to AP.

## M24-FR-03 — Access isolation, document expiry and partner audit trail
- **Shall:** isolate partner access, track document expiry (licences, insurance) and keep a
  partner audit trail.
- **Actors/scope:** Platform/Buyer admin. **Preconditions:** partner accounts.
- **Flow:** partner access scoped + time-bounded → documents tracked with expiry alerts →
  every partner action audited. **Alt/error:** expired document blocks the relevant action.
- **Rules:** least privilege (P-04); **no cross-partner/tenant access** (§35); audit never
  deleted (#6).
- **Permissions:** partner-scoped, audited. **Offline:** online.
- **Audit/privacy:** full partner audit trail. **Integration:** API-11.
- **Acceptance:** a partner sees only its data; an expired licence blocks the action; the
  audit trail is complete.

## M24-FR-04 — API/EDI readiness for high-volume partners
- **Shall:** offer API/EDI integration for high-volume partners.
- **Actors/scope:** Partner developers. **Preconditions:** versioned API/connector SDK (M32).
- **Flow:** partner integrates via versioned API/EDI + sandbox → certified → production.
  **Alt/error:** breaking/unversioned change rejected.
- **Rules:** versioned, idempotent (P-06, M32); sandbox isolated from production (#7).
- **Permissions:** partner API keys scoped/audited. **Offline:** online.
- **Audit/privacy:** API usage audited. **Integration:** API-03/API-11; M32/M36 ecosystem.
- **Acceptance:** a high-volume partner exchanges POs/ASNs/invoices via versioned API/EDI.
