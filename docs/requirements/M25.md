# M25 — Workforce, tasks and enablement

- **Priority:** P3 · **Release:** R6 · **Roadmap:** §5 M25, §16 D11 (workforce/ops), §28. **API-11.**
- **Purpose:** Run the people side of the floor — assignments and authorization, shift/roster and attendance, daily tasks and handover, training/certification/incentives, and role-aware SOP/help — so the right person does the right task and knows how. A per-tenant **optional** feature (ADR-0003).

> Appendix B; §5 inherited. Uses RBAC (`packages/rbac`), approvals and the manager surface
> (Stage-3 `store-manager.md`).

## M25-FR-01 — Employee assignment, authorization, shift/roster, attendance
- **Shall:** manage employee assignment, authorization, shifts/rosters and attendance integration.
- **Actors/scope:** Manager/HR. **Preconditions:** users/roles (M01/M02); `⟳ AVR-08` (roles/manpower).
- **Flow:** assign employees to roles/branches → build rosters → attendance feeds shift
  reports. **Alt/error:** an unstaffed critical role flags an exception.
- **Rules:** authorization via RBAC (least privilege, P-04); joiner-mover-leaver (M02-FR-04);
  no shared logins (#4).
- **Permissions (§28):** managers within scope. **Offline (§31):** rosters/attendance cache;
  sync on reconnect.
- **Audit/privacy:** assignment/attendance audited; staff PII minimized. **Integration:** API-11.
- **Acceptance:** an employee's access matches their assignment; a roster gap is visible;
  attendance ties to shift reports.

## M25-FR-02 — Daily tasks, opening/closing, handover and escalation
- **Shall:** manage daily tasks, opening/closing checklists, shift handover and escalation.
- **Actors/scope:** Manager/Staff. **Preconditions:** task definitions (D11).
- **Flow:** tasks/checklists routed to the right role → completion recorded → handover at
  shift end → overdue/critical items escalate. **Alt/error:** a blocked task escalates (P-03).
- **Rules:** completion is queued offline and synced (Stage-3 manager spec); escalation policy.
- **Permissions:** role-routed. **Offline (§31):** tasks/checklists cache; completion queues.
- **Audit/privacy:** task completion/handover audited. **Integration:** API-11.
- **Acceptance:** opening/closing checklists route and complete offline; an overdue critical
  task escalates.

## M25-FR-03 — Training, certification, productivity and incentives
- **Shall:** track training, certification, productivity and incentives.
- **Actors/scope:** Manager/HR. **Preconditions:** employees (FR-01).
- **Flow:** assign training → record certification → measure productivity → compute
  incentives. **Alt/error:** an expired certification blocks a restricted task (e.g. age-
  restricted sales, food handling).
- **Rules:** certification gates relevant actions; incentives computed with exact money
  (`packages/pricing`).
- **Permissions:** HR/manager. **Offline:** online.
- **Audit/privacy:** training/incentive records audited. **Integration:** API-11.
- **Acceptance:** an expired certification blocks the gated task; incentives compute exactly.

## M25-FR-04 — Role-aware SOP / help centre and acknowledgement
- **Shall:** deliver role-aware SOPs/help and capture acknowledgement.
- **Actors/scope:** All staff. **Preconditions:** roles (M02); SOP content.
- **Flow:** staff see SOPs/help for their role → acknowledge reading → acknowledgement
  recorded. **Alt/error:** unacknowledged critical SOP flagged.
- **Rules:** content role-scoped; acknowledgement auditable.
- **Permissions:** role-scoped. **Offline (§31):** SOP/help cached on device.
- **Audit/privacy:** acknowledgements audited. **Integration:** API-11.
- **Acceptance:** a cashier sees cashier SOPs; acknowledgement is recorded; help works offline.
