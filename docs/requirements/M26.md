# M26 — Facilities, assets and maintenance

- **Priority:** P3 · **Release:** R6 · **Roadmap:** §5 M26, §16 D14 (hardware/IoT), §35. **API-11.**
- **Purpose:** Keep the physical store running — assets/warranty/AMC/service/spares, refrigeration/cold-room/power/UPS/DG with IoT readiness, cleaning/pest/fire/safety/maintenance schedules, and energy/downtime/incident/compliance evidence. A per-tenant **optional** feature (ADR-0003); cold-chain ties to inventory quality (M10).

> Appendix B; §5 inherited.

## M26-FR-01 — Asset, warranty, AMC, service and spare tracking
- **Shall:** track assets with warranty, AMC, service history and spares.
- **Actors/scope:** Facilities/Manager. **Preconditions:** `⟳ AVR-06` (equipment inventory).
- **Flow:** register asset → record warranty/AMC → log service → track spares. **Alt/error:**
  warranty/AMC expiry alerts; a critical asset overdue for service flags.
- **Rules:** asset lifecycle tracked; expiry alerts (like licence expiry, M11/M27 pattern).
- **Permissions:** facilities role. **Offline (§31):** logs cache; sync on reconnect.
- **Audit/privacy:** service/asset changes audited. **Integration:** API-11.
- **Acceptance:** an AMC nearing expiry alerts; a service log ties to the asset.

## M26-FR-02 — Refrigeration/cold-room, power/UPS/DG and IoT readiness
- **Shall:** monitor refrigeration/cold-room and power (UPS/DG), with IoT-sensor readiness.
- **Actors/scope:** Facilities. **Preconditions:** equipment (AVR-06).
- **Flow:** capture temperature/power status (manual now; IoT-ready) → breaches alert →
  cold-chain evidence feeds inventory quality (M10). **Alt/error:** a cold-chain breach
  raises a quality hold (M10).
- **Rules:** cold-chain evidence links to batches (M10); IoT is readiness (D14), not a
  hard dependency.
- **Permissions:** facilities. **Offline (§31):** readings cache/queue.
- **Audit/privacy:** breaches/evidence audited. **Integration:** API-11/API-04.
- **Acceptance:** a cold-room breach alerts and records evidence against affected stock.

## M26-FR-03 — Cleaning, pest, fire, safety and maintenance schedules
- **Shall:** schedule and evidence cleaning, pest control, fire, safety and maintenance.
- **Actors/scope:** Facilities/Manager. **Preconditions:** schedules configured.
- **Flow:** scheduled tasks routed (via M25 tasks) → completion + evidence captured →
  overdue escalates. **Alt/error:** a missed safety task escalates (P-03).
- **Rules:** compliance-linked schedules; evidence retained (audit, #6).
- **Permissions:** facilities role. **Offline (§31):** schedules/evidence cache.
- **Audit/privacy:** completion + evidence audited. **Integration:** API-11.
- **Acceptance:** a due safety check routes, is evidenced, and escalates if missed.

## M26-FR-04 — Energy, downtime, incidents and compliance evidence
- **Shall:** track energy use, equipment downtime, incidents and compliance evidence.
- **Actors/scope:** Facilities/Owner. **Preconditions:** asset/reading data (FR-01/02).
- **Flow:** capture energy/downtime/incidents → report → hold compliance evidence.
  **Alt/error:** an open safety incident is a visible exception.
- **Rules:** evidence sufficient for compliance (like C-01 for finance); retained (#6).
- **Permissions:** facilities/owner. **Offline:** reporting uses last-synced data (freshness shown).
- **Audit/privacy:** incidents/evidence audited. **Integration:** API-11/API-10.
- **Acceptance:** downtime/energy report computes; a safety incident is tracked with evidence.
