# M27 — Concession and shop-in-shop

- **Priority:** P3 · **Release:** R6 · **Roadmap:** §5 M27, §16 D07/D10, §28. **API-09/API-11.**
- **Purpose:** Run concessionaire / shop-in-shop counters — contracts, deposits, rent/revenue-share and utilities; **separate stock ownership** and restricted access; counter-sales settlement/reconciliation; and agreement/insurance/licence expiry alerts. A per-tenant **optional** feature (ADR-0003 entitlement; built only where a store runs concessions, `⟳ AVR-12`).

> Appendix B; §5 inherited. Reuses the append-only ledger, approvals and reconciliation.

## M27-FR-01 — Concessionaire contracts, deposits, rent/revenue share, utilities
- **Shall:** manage concessionaire contracts, deposits, rent/revenue-share and utility charges.
- **Actors/scope:** Owner/Finance, concessionaire. **Preconditions:** `⟳ AVR-12` (does the
  store run concessions?).
- **Flow:** define contract → deposit recorded → rent/revenue-share/utilities computed per
  period. **Alt/error:** an overdue payment flags; an expired contract blocks new sales
  pending renewal.
- **Rules:** revenue-share computed with exact money (`packages/pricing`); deposits are
  liabilities (M23); contract terms governed.
- **Permissions (§28):** contracts approved. **Offline:** finance online.
- **Audit/privacy:** contract/deposit changes audited. **Integration:** API-09.
- **Acceptance:** revenue-share computes exactly; a deposit reconciles as a liability.

## M27-FR-02 — Separate stock ownership and restricted access
- **Shall:** keep concession stock ownership separate and access restricted.
- **Actors/scope:** Concessionaire, store. **Preconditions:** ownership status (M08 D05).
- **Flow:** concession stock tagged with its ownership → excluded from the store's own
  valuation → access restricted to the concessionaire. **Alt/error:** cross-access blocked.
- **Rules:** **ownership status** on the stock ledger (M08 `ownership` field); the store does
  not own concession stock; least-privilege access (P-04).
- **Permissions:** concession-scoped. **Offline (§31):** stock ledger append-only; scoped.
- **Audit/privacy:** access audited. **Integration:** API-04/API-11.
- **Acceptance:** concession stock is excluded from the store's valuation; only the
  concessionaire accesses it.

## M27-FR-03 — Counter sales settlement, invoices and reconciliation
- **Shall:** settle concession counter sales, issue invoices and reconcile.
- **Actors/scope:** Concessionaire/Cashier, Finance. **Preconditions:** POS (M12), contract (FR-01).
- **Flow:** counter sale rung → attributed to the concession → settled per contract →
  reconciled (revenue-share/rent netted). **Alt/error:** a mismatch is a valued exception (P-08).
- **Rules:** sales attributed correctly; settlement reconciles (M23); no card data (#3).
- **Permissions:** concession-scoped. **Offline (§31):** counter sale offline-capable (hard
  rule #1) then settled.
- **Audit/privacy:** settlement audited. **Integration:** API-05/API-09.
- **Acceptance:** a concession sale attributes to the concessionaire and reconciles at
  settlement.

## M27-FR-04 — Agreement / insurance / licence expiry alerts
- **Shall:** alert on agreement, insurance and licence expiry.
- **Actors/scope:** Owner/Finance. **Preconditions:** contract/document dates (FR-01).
- **Flow:** track expiry dates → alert ahead of expiry → block new sales on lapse per policy.
  **Alt/error:** expired agreement blocks the concession pending renewal.
- **Rules:** expiry alerts (shared pattern with M11/M26); compliance-linked.
- **Permissions:** owner/finance. **Offline:** online.
- **Audit/privacy:** alerts/renewals audited. **Integration:** API-11.
- **Acceptance:** an expiring agreement alerts; a lapsed one blocks new sales.
