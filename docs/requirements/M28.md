# M28 — Waste, sustainability and packaging

- **Priority:** P3 · **Release:** R6 · **Roadmap:** §5 M28, §16 D05, §28, §35. **API-04/API-10.**
- **Purpose:** Handle what leaves as loss or waste — wastage/damage/expiry/donation/destruction with approvals, scrap/recycling sales and evidence, carry-bag/reusable-packaging inventory, and waste/energy/sustainability reporting. A per-tenant **optional** feature (ADR-0003). Loss events are compensating stock movements (M08, hard rule #2).

> Appendix B; §5 inherited. Reuses the append-only ledger and approvals.

## M28-FR-01 — Wastage, damage, expiry, donation and destruction approvals
- **Shall:** record wastage/damage/expiry/donation/destruction with reason codes and approval.
- **Actors/scope:** Warehouse/Manager (raise), Manager/Owner (approve). **Preconditions:**
  stock ledger (M08), expiry (M10).
- **Flow:** loss proposed with reason + value → approved by threshold → **compensating stock
  movement appended** (M08-FR-03) → evidence captured (photo/witness). **Alt/error:**
  over-threshold escalates; self-approval of material loss forbidden (§28).
- **Rules:** losses are **compensating events, never edits** (#2); reason codes mandatory;
  value impact recorded (feeds M23).
- **Permissions (§28):** approval above raiser authority. **Offline (§31):** queued; approval
  on sync.
- **Audit/privacy:** every loss audited with reason/value/approver + evidence. **Integration:**
  API-04.
- **Acceptance:** a write-off needs a reason and approval; it appends one compensating
  movement and a valued record.

## M28-FR-02 — Scrap / recycling sales and evidence
- **Shall:** record scrap/recycling sales with evidence.
- **Actors/scope:** Manager/Finance. **Preconditions:** loss/scrap stock (FR-01).
- **Flow:** scrap sold/recycled → proceeds recorded → evidence retained → reconciled (M23).
  **Alt/error:** unevidenced scrap sale flagged.
- **Rules:** proceeds are income (M23); evidence retained (#6).
- **Permissions:** finance/manager. **Offline:** online.
- **Audit/privacy:** scrap sales audited. **Integration:** API-04/API-09.
- **Acceptance:** a scrap sale records proceeds and evidence and reconciles.

## M28-FR-03 — Carry bags, reusable packaging and packaging inventory
- **Shall:** track carry-bag and reusable-packaging inventory (and charges where applicable).
- **Actors/scope:** POS/Inventory. **Preconditions:** product/stock (M03/M08).
- **Flow:** packaging tracked as stock; carry-bag charge applied at POS per policy;
  reusable-packaging returns handled. **Alt/error:** negative packaging stock flagged.
- **Rules:** packaging is stock (M08); carry-bag charge is a priced line (M12/M05) where the
  tenant enables it.
- **Permissions:** standard. **Offline (§31):** POS charge offline-capable.
- **Audit/privacy:** packaging movements audited. **Integration:** API-04/API-05.
- **Acceptance:** carry-bag charge appears on the bill where enabled; packaging stock tracks.

## M28-FR-04 — Waste, energy and sustainability reports
- **Shall:** report waste, energy and sustainability metrics.
- **Actors/scope:** Owner/Manager. **Preconditions:** loss (FR-01), energy (M26-FR-04).
- **Flow:** aggregate waste/energy/sustainability → report with drill-through. **Alt/error:**
  anomalies surface as exceptions.
- **Rules:** derived from events; freshness shown (P-08).
- **Permissions:** owner/manager. **Offline:** last-synced with freshness.
- **Audit/privacy:** report params logged. **Integration:** API-10.
- **Acceptance:** a waste/sustainability report computes from recorded losses and energy.
