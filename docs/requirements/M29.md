# M29 — Owner command centre and BI

- **Priority:** P1/P2 · **Release:** R2 · **Stage:** 10 · **Roadmap:** §5 M29, §16 D13, §32 (freshness), §31
- **Purpose:** Your control, in your hand (P-03). The KPIs that matter, comparisons and drill-through, a **daily brief that reaches your phone with nobody sending it**, and exception alerts pushed to you — always with a data-freshness indicator so you know how current the numbers are.

## M29-FR-01 — Sales, margin, cash, stock, purchase, customer, fulfilment KPIs
- **Shall:** present the core KPIs across sales, margin, cash, stock, purchase, customer and fulfilment.
- **Actors/scope:** Owner/Manager. **Preconditions:** source data (M08/M12/M23 etc.).
- **Flow:** compute KPIs from governed metrics → present on dashboard with freshness. **Alt/error:** stale/missing data shown as such, never as fresh (P-08).
- **Rules:** KPIs use governed semantic definitions (§8.3) so numbers are consistent everywhere; scoped by branch/role (§28).
- **Permissions (§28):** Owner sees all; managers scoped. **Offline (§31):** dashboard shows **last synchronized data only**, with prominent freshness per branch/domain (§31).
- **Audit/privacy:** report access logged. **Integration/reporting:** API-10.
- **Acceptance:** each KPI ties to its source; freshness is shown; a manager sees only their scope.

## M29-FR-02 — Comparisons and drill-through
- **Shall:** compare across branch/category/vendor/staff and drill through from a KPI to the underlying detail.
- **Actors/scope:** Owner/Manager. **Preconditions:** KPIs (FR-01).
- **Flow:** select comparison → view ranked results → drill to transactions. **Alt/error:** drill respects the user's data scope.
- **Rules:** drill-through reaches the immutable source (NFR-15); comparisons use the same governed metrics.
- **Permissions (§28):** scope-enforced drill. **Offline (§31):** last-synced with freshness.
- **Audit/privacy:** drill access logged. **Integration/reporting:** API-10.
- **Acceptance:** a KPI drills to the exact transactions behind it; comparisons reconcile to the KPIs; scope is enforced on drill.

## M29-FR-03 — Freshness indicator, thresholds, alerts and approval inbox
- **Shall:** show data freshness, let the owner set thresholds, push **exception alerts**, and provide an approval inbox.
- **Actors/scope:** Owner. **Preconditions:** approval engine (M02-FR-03), loss-prevention (M15).
- **Flow:** set thresholds → breaches raise alerts pushed to the owner → approvals routed to the inbox for one-tap decision. **Alt/error:** alert storms are grouped; a pending approval invalidated by change is flagged (§31.1).
- **Rules:** **exception alerts** include large discount, voided bill, price override, after-hours login, cash short (roadmap/Annexure H Stage 10); approvals reuse the M02 engine (deterministic commit, AI never commits).
- **Permissions (§28):** owner inbox; approvals bound to authority. **Offline (§31):** alerts/approvals online; freshness shown.
- **Audit/privacy:** alerts and approval decisions audited. **Integration/reporting:** API-10; alert and approval reports.
- **Acceptance:** a voided-bill exception reaches the owner and is real; the owner approves a pending item from the inbox and it commits; freshness is always visible.

## M29-FR-04 — Scheduled reports, exports and daily AI narrative
- **Shall:** schedule reports/exports and deliver a **daily brief** with a plain-language AI narrative (A01, read-only).
- **Actors/scope:** Owner. **Preconditions:** KPIs (FR-01), AI gateway (M32/A01).
- **Flow:** schedule the daily brief at a fixed time → it arrives on the phone with what sold, margin, cash, and the three things needing attention → one tap to the underlying report. **Alt/error:** if AI/model/internet fails, the deterministic brief still sends (AI is additive, never blocking).
- **Rules:** **the daily brief sends automatically with nobody sending it**; AI narrates read-only with evidence/uncertainty (AI-NFR-04); Tamil/English (NFR-08).
- **Permissions (§28):** owner-scoped. **Offline (§31):** AI online only; the underlying report uses last-synced data; the brief degrades gracefully without AI.
- **Audit/privacy:** brief generation and AI narrative logged (accept/reject tracking). **Integration/reporting:** API-10/API-13.
- **Acceptance:** the daily brief arrives on the phone at the set time for three days running without anyone sending it; last month's store P&L is viewable by the owner without asking anyone; if AI is off, the numbers still arrive.

## Module acceptance & test families
- KPI-to-source integrity; scoped drill-through; real exception alerts (large discount/void/override/after-hours/cash short); automatic daily brief with graceful no-AI fallback; freshness always shown.
