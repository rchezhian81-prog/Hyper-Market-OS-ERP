# M34 — Audit, risk and compliance evidence

- **Priority:** P0/P1 · **Release:** R1 · **Stage:** 5 · **Roadmap:** §5 M34, §9 (SEC/PRV), §9.3, §31, NFR-15
- **Purpose:** The tamper-evident memory of the whole system. Every sensitive action is reconstructable from immutable evidence — **not from a screen anyone can change** (NFR-15) — and **audit evidence is never deleted** (hard rule #6). This is what lets you recover trust on the one evening it matters.

## M34-FR-01 — Immutable who/what/when/where/before/after records
- **Shall:** record every sensitive action as an immutable event capturing who, what, when, where, and the before/after state.
- **Actors/scope:** every module writes; Auditor/Owner read. **Preconditions:** IAM (M02).
- **Flow:** a sensitive mutation/approval emits an audit event → stored append-only, tamper-evident. **Alt/error:** any attempt to edit/delete an audit record fails.
- **Rules:** **impossible for any user, including the owner, to edit the audit log** (roadmap Stage 5 / Annexure H); tamper-evident (SEC-07); events reconstruct the action without a mutable screen (NFR-15).
- **Permissions (§28):** write via authorized module actions; **no user deletes/edits** (Auditor is read-only). **Offline (§31):** edge captures audit locally; syncs; never dropped.
- **Audit/privacy:** the audit *is* the evidence; PII classified/masked in views (PRV). **Integration/reporting:** API-11; audit search.
- **Acceptance:** trying to edit or delete an audit record fails (owner check from Stage 5); a sensitive action is fully reconstructable from evidence alone; offline actions appear in the audit after sync.

## M34-FR-02 — Search, export, legal hold and retention
- **Shall:** search and export audit/evidence, apply legal holds, and enforce retention.
- **Actors/scope:** Auditor/Compliance/Owner. **Preconditions:** audit store (FR-01).
- **Flow:** search by actor/object/time → export for audit → apply legal hold (suspends deletion) → enforce retention schedule. **Alt/error:** a record under legal hold cannot be deleted even past retention.
- **Rules:** retention per policy (PRV-08); **defensible deletion with statutory exceptions**; legal hold overrides deletion; **dead-letter/migration exceptions never deleted** (hard rule #6).
- **Permissions (§28):** legal hold by authorized role; auditor export scoped. **Offline (§31):** online.
- **Audit/privacy:** searches/exports/holds themselves audited. **Integration/reporting:** API-11; audit-export and retention reports.
- **Acceptance:** a record under legal hold survives past its retention date; an auditor can export the evidence for a period; retention runs without deleting held or statutorily-required data.

## M34-FR-03 — Licence/certificate register, food-safety, metrology, privacy evidence
- **Shall:** maintain registers of licences/certificates and food-safety, Legal-Metrology and privacy evidence with expiry alerting.
- **Actors/scope:** Compliance/Store Operations. **Preconditions:** compliance obligations (§9.3, `docs/registers/compliance.md`).
- **Flow:** record licences/certificates + evidence → alert before expiry to a named person → keep evidence retrievable. **Alt/error:** an expired licence raises an escalating alert.
- **Rules:** each obligation has a validator role (§9.3); **expiry alerts point at a real person** (Annexure H Stage 16); evidence retained (never deleted).
- **Permissions (§28):** compliance scope. **Offline (§31):** online.
- **Audit/privacy:** compliance evidence immutable/retained. **Integration/reporting:** API-11; licence-expiry and compliance-evidence reports.
- **Acceptance:** an approaching licence expiry alerts a named person; food-safety/metrology/privacy evidence is retrievable for an inspection; nothing in these registers can be deleted.

## M34-FR-04 — Risk, control, incident, remediation, attestation registers
- **Shall:** maintain risk, control, incident, remediation and attestation registers.
- **Actors/scope:** Security/Compliance/Owner. **Preconditions:** governance registers (`docs/registers/`).
- **Flow:** record risks/controls → link incidents to controls → track remediation → capture attestations. **Alt/error:** an open critical risk blocks the relevant gate (QG).
- **Rules:** links controls↔incidents↔remediation; feeds the quality gates (QG-06 security, QG-08 recovery); attestations time-stamped and immutable.
- **Permissions (§28):** security/compliance scope. **Offline (§31):** online.
- **Audit/privacy:** register changes audited. **Integration/reporting:** API-11; risk/incident/attestation reports.
- **Acceptance:** an incident links to its control and remediation; an open critical risk is visible at the relevant gate; attestations are immutable and dated.

## Module acceptance & test families

- Legal hold overrides retention; expiry alerts reach a named person; risk/incident linkage.
