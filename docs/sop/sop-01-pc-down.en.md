# Sheet 1 — The PC is down or the new till has stopped: old till only

**Sheet id:** SOP-01 · **Decision:** OB-255 (owner, 20 Oct 2026) · **Language:** English (Tamil: `sop-01-pc-down.ta.md`)
**For:** everyone at the pilot counter.

**When to use this sheet:** the new till's screen shows an error and will not take an item; the black till window has
closed; the PC is off, frozen or restarting; or a power cut has switched the PC off.

1. Keep billing the customer on the **old till (old ERP)**, as always. The customer's bill always comes from the old
   till.
2. **Stop keying into the new till.** Do not try to fix it: do not restart the PC, do not close or open windows, do not
   unplug anything.
3. Write the **time it stopped** in the table below.
4. Tell the owner, in person or by phone: "The new till stopped at ___."
5. **Do not ring the missed items on the new till later.** The owner records the gap in the evening check.
6. Start keying into the new till again **only when the owner says it is back**. Write that time too.
