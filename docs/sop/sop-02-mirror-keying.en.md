# Sheet 2 — Mirror keying for the pilot department

**Sheet id:** SOP-02 · **Decisions:** OB-248 (mirror pilot), OB-252 (guest sales only), OB-256 (one "paid on old ERP"
payment), OB-258 (refunds rung on both), OB-264 (the old bill number on every sale), OB-269 (exchanges on the old till only), OB-270 (keyed wrongly) · **Language:**
English (Tamil: `sop-02-mirror-keying.ta.md`)
**For:** the trained person at the pilot counter, named by the owner.

**Before you start:** on the new till press **Sign in**, and sign in with **your own** staff ID and **your own** till
PIN. Never use anyone else's. Press **Sign out** whenever you leave the counter. Your PIN opens the till only: the
office screens ask for an office person's PIN. Under its header the new till says **Mirror pilot**. If it does not,
stop: ring nothing on the new till and tell the owner.

**For every customer:**

1. First, bill the customer on the **old till (old ERP)** exactly as today: take the payment as today and give the old
   till's bill. **That is the customer's only bill.**
2. Then, on the **new till**, scan **only the pilot department's items** from that same bill, one by one, in the same
   quantities.
3. **Never give the customer anything from the new till** — not a bill, not a slip.
4. **Guest sale only:** never type a mobile number on the new till, never add loyalty points there. If the customer
   asks for loyalty, it is done on the old till, as today.
5. **Payment on the new till:** press **Tender**. The new till shows one button only: **Paid on old system — ₹(the
   bill's amount)**. Press it.
6. **Old bill number:** the new till asks **Old bill number**. Type the bill number printed on the old till's bill from
   step 1, exactly as it is printed — letters, digits, "-" and "/" only — and press **OK**. Never type a phone number or
   any other detail of the customer. If the till says **That is not an old bill number**, check what you typed, press
   **Tender** again and type it again. If the till says the old bill number **is already keyed on another sale**, check
   the number on the old bill: if you typed it wrong, press **Tender** and type it right; if it is right, this bill was
   already keyed — do not key it twice: tap each of its lines, press **Void**, choose **Scanned twice**, and write it in
   the list below.
7. The till says **Recorded — paid on old system** and "No money in this drawer". Press **OK**. Never put money into
   the new till's drawer and never take money out: the customer paid on the old till. Do not take a float on the new
   till. If the new till offers **Cash**, **Card** or **UPI** instead, stop: the mirror setting is off. Ring nothing
   more on the new till and tell the owner (Sheet 1).

**If the new till says the barcode is not in the catalogue** (no line is added):
- do not pick a similar item, do not type a price, do not scan a different barcode;
- write it in the list below: the time, the barcode number (the digits under the bars), the item's name and the
  quantity from the old bill, and the price on the old bill;
- carry on with the next item. The owner checks this list in the evening.

**If the new till shows a different price from the old bill:** do not change anything. Write it in the list too.

**Refunds (OB-258, OB-264):** do the refund on the **old till** first, as today. Then ring the **same** refund on the
new till, so the stock and the sales still match:
- press **More**, then **Refund**. The till asks **Old bill number on the customer's bill**: type the number printed on
  the customer's old bill and press **OK**. If the till says **No sale on this store computer carries that old bill
  number**, check the number; if it is right, that sale was never keyed on the new till — ring no refund there and
  write it in the list below;
- choose the item coming back, how many, why, and its condition, exactly as on the old till's refund, and type the same
  amount the old till refunded;
- **How is the refund given?** shows one button only: **Paid on old system**. Press it;
- when the till asks for a manager, the manager scans their badge or keys their own staff ID, types **their own** till
  PIN and chooses why. Never type a manager's PIN for them;
- the till says the refund is recorded against the old system's payment. Do not take money from the new till's
  drawer: the refund was given on the old till.

**Exchanges (OB-269):** during the pilot, do exchanges **only on the old till**, as today. Ring nothing on the new
till for an exchange. Write it in the list below (mark it **Exchange**, with the old bill number), so the owner can
explain it in the evening check.

**If you keyed a sale wrongly on the new till (OB-270):** for example a wrong item, a wrong quantity, or the wrong old
bill number. Do not try to change it. Call the manager. On the new till, refund the **whole** wrong sale: **More →
Refund**, type the old bill number you used, choose every line, reason **Other**, and press **Paid on old
system**; the manager approves with **their own** PIN. Then ring the sale again correctly (steps 2 to 7) with the
**right** old bill number. Write both in the list below (mark it **Keyed wrongly**).

**If the new till stops:** use Sheet 1.
