# Sheet 3 — The owner's evening check (parallel-run day)

**Sheet id:** SOP-03 · **Decisions:** OB-254 (the owner is the daily reconciler and the incident owner for the first two
weeks), MG-10 · **Language:** English (Tamil: `sop-03-owner-evening-check.ta.md`)
**For:** the owner, every evening after closing.

> **Before the first day:** the parallel-run terms (who reconciles every day, how many clean days) must be written once,
> with `store-computer parallel-policy` (owner guide Part 23). The new system's figure for each line comes from the
> till's day close, which waits for the new till's "paid on old ERP" payment (Sheet 2, step 5).

**Have ready:** the old ERP's day-end figures **for the pilot department only**; today's Sheet 1 table; today's Sheet 2
list.

1. Read Sheet 1. If the new till stopped today, note the times: the items rung on the old till while it was stopped are
   missing from the new system. That is the reason for those differences.
2. Read Sheet 2's list: each barcode not in the catalogue and each price that differed. Each one explains a difference.
3. In **your own** Windows account, open the migration screen: in Microsoft Edge open the till favourite, and in the
   address bar change the word `pos` at the end to `migration`. Press Enter. The screen says **Enter your PIN**: type
   your own staff ID and your own till PIN, and press **Sign in** (OB-259).
4. Click **Running both**. Under **Enter a day**, set **Which day** to today.
5. For each line type the old ERP's figure and the new system's figure: **Sales (₹)**, **Number of bills**, **Stock
   moved (units)**, **Tax (₹)**, **Payments taken (₹)**, **Loyalty points**.
   - **Loyalty points:** the new till takes no loyalty in the pilot (OB-252), so this line will differ every day. How
     to treat it is **not decided yet** — see the build session's question. Do not invent a reason.
6. Where a line says it differs and **needs a reason**: write in **Why do they differ** what really happened, and choose
   in **Which one was wrong**: the old system, the new system, both, or neither — a timing difference. Never write
   "the new one is probably right".
7. Press **Save the day**.
8. In **What you checked (in your own words)**, write a full sentence, for example "compared the old ERP's day-end
   report for the department with the new till's day close, line by line". Press **Sign the day**.
   - It refuses while any difference has no reason, and names it. Go back to step 6.
9. Press **Sign out** (bottom right of the screen). Put today's Sheets 1 and 2 in the day's folder and initial them.
