# `packages/restricted-sales/`

The till-side restricted-goods gate — **roadmap v2.1 B14** (COTPA 2003 s.6 & s.7): a tobacco
line demands age-18 confirmation and refuses a below-pack quantity (no loose single-stick
sale). Pure and deterministic — the basket lines and the age confirmation go in, the verdict
comes out, and the till commits nothing until it is allowed. Unlocked by the owner's ratified
decision (11 Aug 2026) that tobacco is stocked.
